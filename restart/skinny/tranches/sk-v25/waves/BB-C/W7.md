# BB-C.W7 — optimizer/generator performance apotheosis

## Goal

Re-evaluate every retained complex engine and the whole lowering/generation path
after integration; delete anything whose benefit is not proved.

## Iteration

1. Run paired profiles/benchmarks for direct versus retained e-graph/CSP choices,
   interpreter versus VM/generated faces, cold/warm generation, incremental edits,
   memory, artifact size, and compile/runtime throughput.
2. Audit semantic equivalence, all failure exits, reports, budget behavior,
   provenance, fixed point, and module/graph structure.
3. Dispatch two fresh hostile critics per optimizer/generator cluster and a fresh
   adjudicator. Correct findings and rerun incident/downstream clusters.
4. Require two clean complete audits; statistically clear regressions require
   explanation/correction, not threshold masking.

## Gates

- Every retained e-graph/CSP/specialized generator has a named measured advantage
  and no semantic debt.
- Losing organs and fallback/dual routes are deleted.
- LoweringPlan and artifacts are total, deterministic, and provenance-verified.
- Clean fixed point and all semantic/property/mutation gates pass twice.

## Completion

BB-C closes with the simplest engines that satisfy real semantics and performance.
