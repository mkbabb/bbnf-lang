## §1 — Synthesis

The shortlist is seven candidate waves, under the requested cap of eight. It keeps the accepted Alpha wave shape but narrows the behavior candidates to the converged S-P2 boundaries: W0 telemetry first, W1 CostFacts before behavior, provisional W2 typed product work, W3 Tier A tape plus structural-projection union, W4 direct guard triage, W5 Lock 14 audit, and W6 close. Existing SPEC caps above 90 minutes are not carried as implementation authority here; each redress/implementation slice must be planned at <=90 minutes, or P3-B/P3-F must split the wave before dispatch.

The S-P2 lead is W3 Tier A, not a parser rewrite free-for-all. SC-1 says Tier A attaches the existing `scan_structurals` offset stream, rewrites structural dispatch to indexed cursor reads instead of source-byte re-walk, and explicitly does not claim string-boundary, quote/backslash parity, string-content skipping, or CostFacts-template closure (`SC-1 Tier A/Tier B boundary and owner constraints`). SPEC Section 6 makes the same split, and says `tape_vs_tape` telemetry cannot satisfy W3's production same-wave consumer (`SPEC Section 6 - W3 Tier A Tape Plus Structural-Projection Union`).

The broad Alpha twitter/yyjson retained-parser candidate is not shortlisted as an independent wave because S-P2 narrowed the admissible parse shape to Tier A first. The Alpha candidate's old 900 LOC parser refactor target (`Alpha-E candidate shortlist and pre-blocks`) also violates the current 90-minute implementation cap unless split and re-justified after W0. Bitmap asm bodies are not shortlisted as default behavior: Alpha retained them only as reserve research after fresh profile evidence and challenge acceptance (`SYNTHESIS opening state and S-P2/W3 finding sections`).
