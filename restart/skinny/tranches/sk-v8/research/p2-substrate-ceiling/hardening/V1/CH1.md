3. The main falsifiability gates are not yet executable. SC-1's fusion gate requires self-time to "collapse toward zero" and protects three row families without a numeric threshold or named profile artifact (`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v8/research/p2-substrate-ceiling/SC-1-offset-tape-teardown.md:281-286`). ## §5 — Verdict Rationale

V1 is not correct enough to advance under CH1. The core substrate diagnosis is plausible and several implementation facts resolve cleanly to source lines, but the cohort overreaches from research diagnosis to candidate prescription before current P1/profile evidence exists. It also mixes strict, deferred, sidecar, and SK-V6 historical deltas in the string-density argument, then asks that mixed table to carry admission weight. The falsifiability gates are currently narrative signals, not executable gates with named rows and thresholds. Fold those defects and the cohort can likely move to acceptance; without them, advancing would paper over the exact strictness and evidence failures CH1 is meant to catch.
