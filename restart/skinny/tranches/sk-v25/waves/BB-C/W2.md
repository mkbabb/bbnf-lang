# BB-C.W2 — bounded equality-saturation islands

## Goal

Retain e-graphs only for small semantic languages where congruence and rewrite
equivalence are total, bounded, and measurably useful.

## Implementation

1. Define each candidate island as a closed expression algebra whose constructors
   expose every semantic child. Translation from/to sealed stages is total and
   revision-qualified.
2. Prove every rewrite with exhaustive small-model, property differential, or a
   checked equivalence witness over acceptance/value/span/diagnostic/type.
3. Define deterministic saturation limits and extraction cost; report iterations,
   classes/nodes, applied rules, stop reason, candidates, and chosen proof path.
4. Compare direct normalization from W1 with e-graph output under rule-order/cap/
   hash permutations and on real consumers. Incomplete saturation is a typed
   non-result unless a proved anytime contract exists.
5. Keep only islands with a named semantic or performance win after implementation
   and maintenance cost. Delete the generic ambient integration and losing islands.

## Gates

- All child/congruence deletion mutations fail.
- Translation round-trips and rewrite orders preserve full observations.
- Caps/timeouts/cycles cannot write back partial semantics.
- Retained islands outperform or enable a named corpus with raw evidence.

## Completion

E-graphs, if any, are narrow validated organs rather than architectural authority.
