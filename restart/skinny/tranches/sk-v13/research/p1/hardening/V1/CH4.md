# SK-V13 S-P1 V1 CH4: Cost/Reproducibility

Disposition: REJECT.

CH4 asks whether a third party can reproduce the profile from the method blocks: verbatim commands, run ids, host triple, build flags, and coverage for the profiling/counter surfaces. V1 is not reproducible enough to fold forward as an empirical floor. The artifacts are useful because they disclose their own gaps, but those gaps are blocking: one assigned profiling mode has no capture, direct samply reports wrapper success while profiling a panic path, build provenance is incomplete in multiple artifacts, and requested branch/L1/LLC PMU fields are absent from the exported ledger.
