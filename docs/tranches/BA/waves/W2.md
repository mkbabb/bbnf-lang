The transitional `pub use bbnf_ir::TypeDesc as Layout;` aliases survive only the W2 close artefact; BA.W6's PROGRESS commit verifies the aliases retire by BB.W0.

The Era V failure mode is mitigated because every god-module split has a same-wave consumer — the existing call-sites that `use` the symbol. The splits are mechanical (move definitions, re-export from `mod.rs`); no new substrate without consumer. The layout-lowering rename has the entire downstream codebase as same-wave consumer; W2's `cargo check` is the gate.

The split discipline is per-cohesive-concern, not per-line-count. Per `feedback_no_god_modules` ("every level (crate, module, file) separates concerns; 'utils'/'helpers'/'common' kitchen sinks are god modules in gestation"), the W2 splits create directories whose children represent peer partitions of the parent's concern — no `utils.rs` or `helpers.rs` siblings.
