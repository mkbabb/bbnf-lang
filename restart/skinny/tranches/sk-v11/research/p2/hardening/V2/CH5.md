## Actionable S-P3 Guardrails

1. Every shortlisted packet must copy the V2 output-plane declaration: generated
   Track 1 path, independent Track 2 or oracle path, same-output proof, and an
   explicit no-shared-sidecar/no-Track-1-call assertion.

2. Any byte-class, movemask, string, whitespace, container, or digit packet must
   consume its scalar/SIMD result in the same live loop. Retaining masks,
   positions, cursor state, class lanes, density tables, or decoded scratch is a
   CH5 REJECT.

3. `tape_sparse_flag_delta_lane` may be shortlisted only as an internal
   encoding replacement for existing tape facts and only with a retained-view or
   typed consumer that actually observes `flags_at(cursor)`.

4. Non-JSON proof must land through generated direct/typed parser output or a
   declared host/oracle path. Existing old non-JSON runtime modules and the
   current JSON-provider codegen are cautionary inventory, not acceptable
   templates.

5. Digest/hash work remains outside parser substrate. A digest packet must stay
   in benchmark/oracle or product-host-sink space and prove strict same-output
   equivalence without storing semantic facts for later parser use.
