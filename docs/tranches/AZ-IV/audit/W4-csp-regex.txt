When the upstream structural detectors authoritatively populated a
key-dispatch sidecar (`key_dispatch_configs` / `keyword_branches`),
`build_alt_domain` now returns a singleton domain `{KeyDispatch}`
with the configured dispatch cost. The variable becomes a unit-domain
pin without invoking branch-and-bound search.

Pre-change failure mode: the `dispatch::install` constraint was
unsatisfiable (`KeyDispatch` not in domain), the CSP fell through to
`decode_fallback`, and the consumer's sidecar-override at
`alt_strategy.rs:163-184` was the de-facto authority. With the
override retired, the singleton-domain encoding makes the structural
fact authoritative inside the CSP itself.

The supplementary `decode_fallback` path (used when the CSP is
provably UNSAT) also gains the `sidecar_keys` parameter so the
deterministic fallback honours the same authority. New signature:
`fallback_alt_mode(fact, has_dispatch, sidecar_keys) -> AltMode`.

T9 Divergence — Sidecars Are NOT Parallel
------------------------------------------
The dispatch instructions named T9 ("collapse `recognizer_decisions`
+ `key_dispatch_configs` sidecars into one unified `ir.decisions`
stream"). After inspection the two sidecars encode disjoint
concepts:

- `recognizer_decisions: HashMap<NodeId, RecognizerDecision>` carries
  the CSP-chosen STRATEGY ENUM
  (`{AltMode, WrapMode, RegexEngine}`). One `RecognizerDecision`
  variant per family.

- `key_dispatch_configs: HashMap<NodeId, KeyDispatchMatch>` carries
  the EMISSION PAYLOAD — a struct with keyword strings, separator
  literals, fallback indices, regex scanner ID, dispatched-byte set.
  Consumed by `crates/core/src/backend/driver/alt.rs:201-256` to
  emit the actual key-dispatch code.

Encoding the payload struct as a value in a strategy-decision
stream would either (a) widen the strategy enum to carry the
payload, defeating the strategy/payload separation, or (b) keep
the payload elsewhere, leaving the unified stream as a strategy
sidecar by another name.

The W4.2 invariant the override-retire enforces is the correct one:
"CSP authority must hold post-selection — sidecars carry payload,
not strategy." That invariant is now satisfied without merging the
two streams. T9 unification HALTED per the empty-return rule
("if T9 unification reveals the two sidecars are NOT actually
parallel ..., HALT with the divergence named").
