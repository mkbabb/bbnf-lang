## Scope

1. Consume the W0 Audit-A inventory; for each of the 12 TRANSPOSE items:
   - **closed-by-BA**: cite BA's commit + artefact; verify closure persists at W4 open.
   - **closed-by-BB**: cite BB's commit + artefact; verify closure persists at W4 open.
   - **closed-by-mechanism**: cite the mechanism (e.g., direct-projection eliminated the runtime indirection that the item targeted); verify the mechanism's evidence.
   - **route-to-W4-with-named-criterion**: implement the transposition in W4. Each transposition is a focused refactor; the wave allocates one sub-unit per non-trivial transposition (up to 5 parallel agents).
2. The 12 items are not enumerated here; they live in `docs/tranches/BC/audit/W0-audit-a-inventory.md`. The W4 dispatch reads the inventory and assigns sub-units to the route-to-W4 items.
3. Substrate-audit GREEN at W4 close: every transposition's substrate has a wave-bound consumer.

## Triumvirate Dispatch

Dispatch a triumvirate if any of these occurs:

- the route-to-W4 list is > 5 items at W0 close (sub-unit count exceeds parallel ceiling; the wave splits across W4a + W4b);
- a transposition surfaces an architectural decision the wave cannot make alone (e.g., the transposition requires a public API change BD will own);
- a closed-by-BA or closed-by-BB row regresses (a BA or BB commit got reverted between letter close and BC W4 open; route back to BA / BB);
- a closed-by-mechanism claim cannot verify (the mechanism evidence is missing; the closure is fictional);
- three diagnostic-loop iterations fail to isolate why a transposition breaks a test;
- JSONL transcript quiet >15 minutes;
- first-pass return with no commit and no evidence (after one verbatim redispatch).
