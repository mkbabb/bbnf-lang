no-behavior proof. W4 cannot admit CSS. W5 must prove typed equality before
W6 measures speed. W10 is optional in content but not in disposition: if no

not a paper close.

## Section 4 - Pre-Blocked Routes

Pattern H destructive replacement, CSS legacy proof retirement, and native
primitive wiring all require replacement proof no later than the retire/delete
wave. No retained sidecar, public `UnionTape`, second tape, parser-owned
structural projection, x86 path, FNV production proof, or CSS broadcast proof
can enter the graph.
