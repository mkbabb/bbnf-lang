data loss, and commit the proven campaign in coherent evidence-bearing slices.

1. Verify every skinny carrier has landed or been proved vacuous; delete the entire
   skinny product plane, its fake solvers/scanners/reports/gates, and now-unused

2. Delete manual WASM/generated copies, old parser/type/IR/runtime routes, disabled

3. Re-census all worktrees. Group by unique HEAD and dirty content, inspect each
   individually, and write KEEP/PORT/ARCHIVE/DELETE-CANDIDATE. Never bulk-delete or
   conflate equal paths across HEADs; destructive cleanup needs explicit target

4. Run complete clean omega gates after deletion and verify graph/inventory/ledger
   reconciliation, fixed points, package/browser journeys, and two clean audits.
5. Use the local commit-discipline skill: inspect dirty/staged state, preserve owner
   work, stage only coherent slices, use concrete Conventional Commit scopes, and
   give broad/gate/deletion/history commits evidence-bearing bodies.
6. Publish the final resurrection ledger with every bank, lock, tail item, owner
   gate, prototype, artifact, package, worktree, deletion, hash, test, and commit.

- Inventory has no unowned node, stale artifact, dual route, or unresolved carrier.
- Owner `playground/vite.config.ts` and test-result changes remain preserved unless
