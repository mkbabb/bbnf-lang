# Tranche B5 — Gestalt Substrate Restoration

## Opening

Six waves. Net negative LOC. The B-series restored runtime correctness
through nine architectural changes; the substrate now carries five
smells the user's "no quick solutions, no workarounds, idiomatic
gestalt" directive demands eliminating before AY-II.W1 dispatches.
B5 retires that residual debt: one substrate, named correctly; one
rollback primitive; one position accessor in place of the boundary
leak; god modules decomposed along natural concern boundaries; the
ten remaining test failures closed at root cause, not band-aided.
The gestalt move is W1; W0 prepares the floor; W2-W4 derive from
W1's substrate; W5 closes and threads cross-tranche references.

## Architectural thesis

Five invariants this tranche enforces. They compose: each follows
from the prior, and the whole is the post-B4 substrate the rest of
the roadmap presupposes.

1. **One substrate, named correctly.** `FusedBuilder` is a struct
   that owns a `Tape<R>` plus value-side fields (`value_frames`,
   `value_payloads_narrow`, `value_payloads_wide`,
   `value_open_stack`). The naming admits the weld. Promote the
   value-side state into `Columns`; delete `FusedBuilder` (~1258
   LOC) outright. The substrate is `Tape<R>` over `Columns`.
2. **One rollback primitive.** Three exist today —
   `FusedBuilder::rollback_to`, `Columns::rollback_to`,
   `Columns::truncate` — each documented as "the canonical" with
   carve-outs. After W1, only `Columns::rollback_to(open_offset)`
   remains; `AltLinear` walker routes through it; `truncate`
   either disappears or narrows to `pub(crate)`.
3. **No boundary leak.** `FusedBuilder::columns_mut()` exposes
   `&mut Columns` to generated parsers. CSS L4 alone holds 1061
   callsites of `.columns_mut().len()` in lieu of a `.position()`
   accessor; the leak forced `frame_depth: Vec<u8>` and
   `current_depth: u8` migrations into `Columns`. Introduce
   `Tape::position() -> u32`; the regen sweep retires the escape
   hatch at every callsite.
4. **No `extern crate self as bbnf;`.** The self-alias at
   `crates/core/src/lib.rs:6` is a smell with concrete cause:
   two-substrate mediation. Removing it is a consequence of W1's
   transposition, not an independent task.
5. **No god modules.** `crates/core/src/lower/expression.rs` (2140
   LOC), `backend/rust/emitter/shapes/{dispatcher,inline,flat}.rs`
   (~5000 LOC combined), `crates/tape/src/columns.rs` (1287 LOC
   mixing substrate, reducers, and SIMD), and
   `backend/rust/emitter/grammar.rs` (1286 LOC) all exceed natural
   responsibility boundaries. W3 splits them per
   `feedback_no_god_modules`; no file in modified directories
   exceeds 800 LOC after the wave.

These five compose. Invariant 1 begets invariant 4 (the self-alias
mediates the weld); invariant 1 enables invariant 2 (collapse the
triplication once the surface is unified); invariant 3 retires the
last consumer of the welded surface; invariant 5 is the second-order
consequence — once boundaries are correct, modules sized to
responsibility rather than to history.

   every wave close. Generated files are output of fresh regen.

   close. Iteration time stays within the budget set by AT.

   median within 5% of B4 baseline (2.831 ms)** at W1 close, and
   non-regressing thereafter. The substrate transposition is not
   permitted to bloat the hot path.
5. **`rg -nF 'FusedBuilder|FusedOutput|ValueFramesOutput|columns_mut|frame_depth_mut|extern crate self as bbnf'`** returns
   zero matches outside `Columns` itself after W1 lands.
6. **No file in `crates/core/src/lower/`,
   `crates/core/src/backend/rust/emitter/shapes/`, or
   `crates/tape/src/` exceeds 800 LOC** after W3.
7. **No `helpers.rs`/`utils.rs`/`common.rs` kitchen-sink modules**
   anywhere in the splits W3 produces.
8. **No `#[allow(...)]` introduced** by any wave in this tranche.
   No legacy code, no backward-compat shim, no deferral. The
   directive is unambiguous.
