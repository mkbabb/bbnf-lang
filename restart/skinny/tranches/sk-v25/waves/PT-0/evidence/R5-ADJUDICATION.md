## Finding-by-finding rulings

### A-1 — correct in its bounded core; merged with B-F3

R5 hashes one executable entry file, not the effective non-system runtime code
closure. The distinction is concrete:

- Node's recorded 68,384-byte entry executable is `08dad058…`, while it loads
  `libnode.147.dylib` at `0afe378c904a35194671ca1b19c7e1df8947fd1da01a7ce2efa1576d9ce21b10`
  plus Homebrew libraries. That digest is absent from the receipt.
- rustc's recorded 412,504-byte entry executable is `95d199b3…`, while its
  compiler implementation is the 204,375,512-byte
  `librustc_driver-0abf9245e2d34e96.dylib` at
  `16cd7da56e561db76e509b93edb419e10e90c645014e13a57e7e0aac0ea1f850`.
  That digest and a selected-sysroot ledger are absent.

The recorded entry-file and version hashes remain true and retain that narrow
meaning. It would be overbroad to require arbitrary path hashes for every

The bounded missing proof is the non-system Mach-O closure, the selected Rust
sysroot/component closure, and one Apple platform/code-signing identity for the
system tail. A-1 is therefore correct without adopting its limitless “all OS
state” wording.
