## §2 — Critical findings

### REJECT — alpha-E §6 C-4 owner-paths admit tape-surface edits without Lock-1 triad

`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v14/research/alpha/alpha-E-candidate-shortlist.md:392-411` enumerates the owner paths for C-4, including:

```
- `skinny/crates/runtime/src/tape/{mod,assembler,event_grammar}.rs` —
  same-tape union variants (NOT a new sidecar; per pre-block P-7).
```

The parenthetical disclaimer is the right intent, but Lock 1's 2026-05-21 substrate-ceiling fold (`restart/locks/LOCKS.md:73-82`) demands that every e-graph candidate, backend rewrite, imported scanner plan, union candidate, and SIMD consumer declare `substrate_target` ∈ {`local_temp_only`, `existing_tape`, `direct_sink`, `admitted_fact_output`}, `retention_lifetime` ∈ {`local_loop`, `generated_function`, `output_row`}, and `policy_owner` ∈ {`generated_grammar`, `caller_data`, `none`}. C-4 wires CSP-selected shape into codegen + lowering + runtime; each shape the solver may pick is a union candidate per Lock 1. The owner-path expansion authorises tape-module edits in the same wave without naming per-shape triad declarations. A wave landing this candidate could honestly believe it is honouring Lock 1 (the parenthetical reads that way) while emitting a `runtime::tape::assembler::` variant whose `substrate_target` is implicitly `existing_tape` but whose actual retention is unclear (does the variant retain a cursor list? a class stream? a mask vector?). The opacity is the hidden-coupling vector P-7 names but cannot detect after the fact.

Required fold for V2: C-4's owner-paths section appends per-shape triad-declaration discipline. The same-wave consumer plan adds a `substrate_target=existing_tape | retention_lifetime=generated_function | policy_owner=generated_grammar` triple as a required column in the wave's REDRESS entry for every CSP-selectable shape. Any shape whose triple cannot be declared at wave-plan time abrogates per `[abrogate-before-patch]` and falls under the architectural-block escalation path C-4 already names at lines 649-651.

The disposition is REJECT (not REVISE) because the language as written would licence a parallel-substrate slip without orchestrator visibility; the V2 fold must restate the owner-paths block with the triad discipline named explicitly.

### REVISE — SYNTHESIS §4 G-SIMD-GRAMMAR-POLICY clause omits Lock-1 triad

```
- any SPEC wave that wires `bbnf-simd` into CSS, union, JSON
  `parse_only`, or shared generated code carries `G-SIMD-GRAMMAR-POLICY`
  per `sk-v13/SYNTHESIS.md §4`: consuming-grammar quote/escape/control
  policy or no-string policy, scalar parity, checkasm/differential
  coverage, same-wave measured row consumption, no public substrate API,
  no retained sidecar classifier state;
```

This clause faithfully carries the SK-V13 §4 binding (`sk-v13/SYNTHESIS.md:226-231`). The SK-V14 reformulation should additionally enforce Lock 1's `substrate_target` / `retention_lifetime` / `policy_owner` triple per `LOCKS.md:73-79`, because SK-V14 promotes W8 + W9 to load-bearing — the very mechanism that selects which SIMD primitive a shape consumes. Without the triple, a downstream wave could wire a SIMD producer whose retention is `output_row` (admitted fact output) while the consuming shape's runtime retains a parser-owned cursor — the retained-sidecar smuggle Lock 1 closes. The triple is the discriminator.

Required fold for V2: append to §4 clause — "every SIMD consumer wired by C-4 declares `substrate_target`, `retention_lifetime`, and `policy_owner` per `LOCKS.md:73-82`; xtask gate-json rejects any row whose REDRESS lacks the triple."

### REVISE — SYNTHESIS §2 telemetry needs explicit Track-2-entry-point column

`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v14/SYNTHESIS.md:210-212` records `Track 1 Mbps` / `Track 2 Mbps` and a new `comparator_plane` column. P-7 (`SYNTHESIS.md:127-130`) and `alpha-C §2 P-7` (`alpha-C-redress-digest.md:342-352`) require Track 1 and Track 2 to instantiate distinct sink types, distinct buffer ownership, and distinct dispatch paths. The current telemetry schema has no column proving this — a future wave could wire Track 2 through a shared dispatcher and the misbinding would only surface in a manual harness inspection, not in `xtask gate-json`'s automated check.

Required fold for V2: append a `track2_entry_point` column to the SK-V14 RESULTS schema; `xtask gate-json` rejects any row where the Track 1 and Track 2 entry-point symbol paths share a common ancestor in `runtime::tape::` beyond the public `Tape` / `OffsetFlags` types. The check is mechanical (symbol-table inspection) and closes the P-7 falsifiability gap CH5 is chartered to close.

Required fold for V2: append to HANDOFF §7 verbatim Lock 1 clause — "REVISE any plan that proposes `UnionTape`, a second tape, a public substrate API, a retained class/mask stream, or parser-owned cursor/list state; only G-Omega may amend Lock 1's substrate-union closure."

### REVISE — alpha-C §2 P-7 falsifiability gate weak on buffer ownership

`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v14/research/alpha/alpha-C-redress-digest.md:348-352`:

```
**Falsifiability:** Per-wave audit (S-P0 lens) inspects the bench harness
for shared mutable state between Track 1 and Track 2 invocations; any
shared sink, shared buffer, or shared dispatcher fails the gate. xtask
`gate-json` reads a per-row column naming Track 2's distinct entry point and
rejects rows where Track 1 and Track 2 entry points share a symbol.
```

"Share a symbol" is the strongest mechanical check but covers only the dispatch path. A renamed scanner exposing a fresh symbol while pointing at a shared `OffsetFlags` buffer would pass the gate. The CH5 charge in `CHALLENGE-CONTEXT.md:160` includes "no renamed-scanner Lock 1 violation" verbatim; the gate must mechanically detect renamed-shared-buffer patterns. The cleanest check is at the type level: assert at compile time that `Track1::Sink` and `Track2::Sink` are distinct types, and at runtime that the buffer addresses (via `as *const _ as usize`) for `Track1::tape()` and `Track2::tape()` differ.

Required fold for V2: strengthen the falsifiability gate to a triple check — (a) distinct symbol paths (current), (b) distinct concrete `Sink` types (compile-time `TypeId` inequality), (c) distinct buffer addresses at the first bench iter (runtime address inequality recorded into the per-iter equality column).

### REVISE — alpha-E §6 C-4 same-wave consumer plan permits attribution-only proof

Same finding as the REJECT but at the consumer-plan layer rather than owner-paths. Hot-leaf attribution change is necessary but not sufficient for hidden-coupling decoupling. A renamed scanner producing a fresh symbol would change attribution while violating Lock 1. The samply trace must inspect the symbol's module path, not merely the leaf name.

Required fold for V2: append to the C-4 same-wave consumer plan — "the post-wave hot leaf's module path traces to `runtime::tape::` (an existing same-tape variant) or to a generator-emitted module whose template provenance is named in REDRESS; module paths under `runtime::ext::`, `runtime::sidecar::`, `runtime::union::`, or `runtime::cursor::` are REJECT pre-emptively."
