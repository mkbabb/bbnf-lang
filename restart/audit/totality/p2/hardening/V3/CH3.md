## Verdict

ACCEPT.

V3 preserves the V2 REDRESS guardrails while adding provenance and executable
ledger repairs. I found no V3 dossier that reopens a previously rejected route
as viable without a material differential. REDRESS-119 direct rows, retained
union, PMULL/CSSC, JSON/direct digest and numeric routes, SIMD/string/digit
helpers, and parse-that scanner imports remain candidates only until a fresh
row-local consumer, strict oracle, scalar/checkasm or parity proof, rollback
path, and measured row movement or measured rejection exist.

## Findings

1. V3 does not replace the accepted V2 REDRESS contract. The V3 addendum states
   that it supplements, not replaces, the V2 Lock 14, Lock 1, REDRESS,
   material-differential, and anti-paper-close contracts
   (`restart/audit/totality/p2/T-P2-V3-FOLD-ADDENDUM.md:11`). That matters
   because the V2 addendum is still the binding source for the REDRESS-119
   direct reopen matrix and the Union/PMULL/CSSC material-differential checklist
   (`restart/audit/totality/p2/T-P2-V2-FOLD-ADDENDUM.md:99`,
   `restart/audit/totality/p2/T-P2-V2-FOLD-ADDENDUM.md:120`).

2. REDRESS-119 remains fenced as row-local candidate history, not as a reopened
   direct close. REDRESS 119 itself closed SK-V11 as a measured direct fixpoint,
   made no behavior or `RESULTS.md` row movement, and admitted no direct row
   (`skinny/REDRESS.md:3497`, `skinny/REDRESS.md:3524`). REDRESS 120 then says
   every residual direct row has measured uncloseable/fixpoint proof and remains
   exhausted unless a future pass names a material differential with fresh
   profile and micro-proof evidence (`skinny/REDRESS.md:3542`,
   `skinny/REDRESS.md:3549`). V3 keeps that rule: 2A says each JSON direct
   reopen must cite the addendum's row-specific differential, consumer, strict
   comparator/oracle, and old-route block condition
   (`restart/audit/totality/p2/2A-sota-landscape.md:108`), while 2F says direct
   digest work cannot close by changing comparator meaning and must retain strict
   Track 1/Track 2/serde/sonic equality
   (`restart/audit/totality/p2/2F-parse-that-gaps.md:106`,
   `restart/audit/totality/p2/2F-parse-that-gaps.md:227`).

3. Union and retained structural substrates are still blocked unless materially
   different. REDRESS 96 and 97 both passed correctness/parity gates before
   regressing every required retained-union row, and REDRESS 98 retired the
   union-substrate thesis for those shapes (`skinny/REDRESS.md:2823`,
   `skinny/REDRESS.md:2881`, `skinny/REDRESS.md:2910`). V3 carries that
   forward: 2A rejects using simdjson stage 1 as a retained class-column excuse
   and requires a fresh material differential (`2A-sota-landscape.md:99`), 2B
   rejects class-column, streaming-cursor, parser-owned sidecar, and `UnionTape`
   replay unless the checklist passes (`2B-primitive-vocabulary.md:236`), 2D
   says D3 unblocks the category but not the historical implementations
   (`2D-cost-model.md:171`), and 2F rejects retained scanner substrates while
   allowing only transient scanner consumers (`2F-parse-that-gaps.md:99`,
   `2F-parse-that-gaps.md:160`).

4. PMULL/CSSC remains source-backed but non-admitting. REDRESS 88 rejected the
   PMULL prefix-XOR body after JSON row regressions despite correctness and
   disasm proof, and REDRESS 89 rejected the narrowed CSSC CTZ/bulk consumer
   after guard-row regressions (`skinny/REDRESS.md:2510`,
   `skinny/REDRESS.md:2573`). V3 does not paper-demote those rejections. The
   shared ledger keeps `pmull_cssc_structural_union_emit64` at
   `source_backed`, with `material-differential checklist not passed` and
   abrogates on replay of REDRESS 88/89/96/97/98, missing row consumer, parity
   failure, or guard regression
   (`restart/audit/totality/p2/T-P2-V3-FOLD-ADDENDUM.md:95`). 2B and 2E repeat
   that PMULL/CSSC can reopen only with a new row consumer that deletes or
   bypasses the old scalar cost source and passes strict gates
   (`2B-primitive-vocabulary.md:154`, `2B-primitive-vocabulary.md:290`,
   `2E-host-arch-esoterica.md:82`, `2E-host-arch-esoterica.md:174`).

5. REDRESS 121-127 are still split into prerequisite, comparator, candidate,
   production-split, and one admitted CSS row. The V2 taxonomy remains live:
   121 does not close Lock 14, 122 is prerequisite-only, 123/124 are scaffold and
   comparator infrastructure, 125 admits only through 127, 126 is microbench
   route-production split, and 127 admits one CSS row while union/ASM remain
   routed (`restart/audit/totality/p2/T-P2-V2-FOLD-ADDENDUM.md:138`). V3 mirrors
   that in 2A (`2A-sota-landscape.md:106`), 2B
   (`2B-primitive-vocabulary.md:219`), and the shared ledger: `escape_mask_64`
   is `checkasm_backed` and prerequisite-only, `ascii_set_member64_css_delimiter`
   is `micro_proven` with production consumer missing, and string/digit/escape
   candidates remain below admission until consumed by JSON/CSS rows
   (`restart/audit/totality/p2/T-P2-V3-FOLD-ADDENDUM.md:91`,
   `restart/audit/totality/p2/T-P2-V3-FOLD-ADDENDUM.md:92`,
   `restart/audit/totality/p2/T-P2-V3-FOLD-ADDENDUM.md:93`,
   `restart/audit/totality/p2/T-P2-V3-FOLD-ADDENDUM.md:94`,
   `restart/audit/totality/p2/T-P2-V3-FOLD-ADDENDUM.md:96`).

6. No prior guard is demoted by paperwork. V3 normalizes admission states so
   `source_backed`, `scalar_backed`, `checkasm_backed`, and `micro_proven` do
   not imply admission, while `conditional`, `inventory`, `ADMITTED-EVIDENCE`,
   and `NOT-VALIDATED` are blockers or historical evidence only
   (`restart/audit/totality/p2/T-P2-V3-FOLD-ADDENDUM.md:56`). The numeric caps
   reject row regressions, parity/equality failures, stale cost evidence, and

   and normalized state (`2B-primitive-vocabulary.md:141`,

   `2F-parse-that-gaps.md:188`).
