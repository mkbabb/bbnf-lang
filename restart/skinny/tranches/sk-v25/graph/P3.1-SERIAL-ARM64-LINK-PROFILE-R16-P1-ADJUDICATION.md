## Correct Apple `time -l` semantics

Critic B's primary-source distinction controls. Apple
[`time.c`](https://github.com/apple-oss-distributions/shell_cmds/blob/298787009e5432c5e4c378a077f98267077e3495/time/time.c)
forks/execs the immediate Cargo child, obtains `proc_pid_rusage(pid,
RUSAGE_INFO_V4)` for that Cargo PID, and separately obtains `struct rusage`
from `wait4(pid, ...)`.

The block is mixed, not homogeneous:

| Printed fields | Source and truthful meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `real` | Parent monotonic interval around the immediate child. |
| `user`, `sys`, maximum RSS through involuntary context switches | The `wait4` rusage returned for Cargo. XNU exit/reap folds already reaped child usage into ancestors. CPU times and ordinary counters are accumulated; `ru_maxrss` is max-reduced. It is not an RSS sum, process timeline, PID attribution, or necessarily every daemon/helper causally associated with the build. |
| instructions retired, cycles elapsed, peak memory footprint | `proc_pid_rusage` fields sampled for the immediate Cargo PID. They are not taken from the `wait4` rollup. XNU exposes separate child fields, but `time` does not print those as these three rows. |

Apple XNU's
[`ruadd`](https://github.com/apple-oss-distributions/xnu/blob/f6217f891ac0bb64f3d375211650a4c1ff8ca1ea/bsd/kern/kern_resource.c)
max-reduces `ru_maxrss` and adds the remaining ordinary range, while the
[`kern_exit.c`](https://github.com/apple-oss-distributions/xnu/blob/f6217f891ac0bb64f3d375211650a4c1ff8ca1ea/bsd/kern/kern_exit.c)
exit/reap paths roll a child's already combined rusage into its parent.

Consequently:

- `2,981,249,024` maximum RSS is a max-reduced high-water observation in the
  successful P1 hierarchy, not additive simultaneous memory and not the old
  killed linker's identified peak;
- `79,217,312` peak footprint is the immediate Cargo `proc_pid_rusage` field,
  so its difference from maximum RSS is not a contradiction;
- 244 signals is an accumulated delivery count, not 244 SIGKILLs and not a
  sender/target receipt;
- zero swaps or block operations does not disprove memory pressure or
  persistent sccache-daemon I/O; and
- none of the block identifies a historical R15 signal sender or cause.

The author and Critic A are incorrect where they describe the whole block as
one descendant-tree aggregate. Critic B is sustained on this issue.
