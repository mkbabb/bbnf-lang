# S-P1 V1 CH3 Regression

Disposition: ACCEPT.

The artefacts do not reopen REDRESS-blocked routes. Numeric, decoded-string,
structural-stream, string64, fixed-shape Unicode, retained cursor, sidecar,
and native SIMD paths are named as constrained or blocked unless a fresh S-P2
primitive and same-wave gate consume them. P1-C explicitly excludes the x86 PEXT
probe on Apple M5 Max.
