## §1 — Synthesis (concrete; cites P1 row, P2 candidate, REDRESS entry, goalset line)

The SK-V17 SPEC is bounded by three facts the upstream passes locked:

1. **The recognizer already BEATS lightningcss; the gap is materialization, not recognition.**
   S-P1 V4 §3.1 measures `track1_full_parse` at 2.01–3.09× lightningcss full-CSSOM
   (bootstrap 2.05×, tailwind 3.09×, material 2.05×, animate 2.01×, all N=200 cold
   median). The loss plane is `track1_fact_stream` at 0.60–0.77× — a 4.4× instr/byte
   tax (215–365 i/B vs 46–58 i/B, §3.1 PMU table). The SK-V17 subject is therefore to
   land a *typed, preserve-rich-ast* plane at full-parse-like cost by killing the
   fact-stream String and decoding the tape lazily — NOT to chase recognition speed
   (already won) and NOT to materialize eagerly (AZ-IV K-blocked, SYNTHESIS §0.4).

2. **The lever order is fixed by the profile: TAPE FIRST, THEN NEON.** S-P1 V4 §3.4(2)
   states the NEON scan leaf (`find_component_delim` 59.24% + `consume_balanced_at`
   10.31% = ~69% recognition self-time) is *masked by the String alloc floor on the
   typed plane* and "S-P2 must not invert it." There is no structural index to pre-scan
   into until the tape decodes CSS (SYNTHESIS §0.1 NEON gate). So the wave topology is
   forced: prune the String + activate the tape (W1) → lazy projection consumer (W2) →
   NEON on the surviving scan (W3) → conditional commit-by-construction (W4).

3. **The four-lever stack maps the locked candidate pool onto waves with no orphans.**
   SYNTHESIS §3 trajectory: (1) kill fact-stream String via `TapeBuilder` append +
   retire `W5C_REQUEST_FACT_PROFILES`; (2) one-shot capacity reserve; (3) NEON
   structural pre-scan re-profiled on the benched path; (4) commit-by-construction.
   The candidate-to-wave assignment (every wave consumes its primitive same-commit,
   PASS-3 §8.5):

   | Wave | Candidates | Lever | S-P1 antecedent |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | W1 PRUNE+activate | L2 `push_plain_offset` · L7 one-shot reserve · L3 lazy `ValueRef` (the consumer) | levers 1+2 | `emit_fact_stream` 25.01% + ~64% alloc floor (§3.3) |
   | W2 projection generalize | L3 (full rider) · L8 sparse-flag · L4 tokenize-once | layout-driven projection | the String materialization floor; 2–3× re-walk (§3.3 P1-D note) |
   | W3 NEON structural index | L1 eq-set classifier · L5 comment mask · L6 bracket mask | lever 3 | `find_component_delim`+`consume_balanced_at` ~69% (§3.3), RE-PROFILED on tape path |
   | W4 commit-by-construction (CONDITIONAL) | L9 Alt-mode | lever 4 | recognition-control loop 28.87%+2.45% — GATED on post-W1 typed re-profile (§3.3, L9 condition) |

   L2/L3 are paired (L3 IS L2's consumer, P2 §L3 same-wave-consumer); L1's `Vec<u32>`
   index IS the tape's `offsets` (the L1/L4 identity carry-forward condition §6.1). No
   primitive lands without its hot-path consumer in the same commit (no-deferrals;
