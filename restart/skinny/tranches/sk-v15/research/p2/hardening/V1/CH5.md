## Governing Criteria

CH5 rejects hidden substrate splits: no parallel substrate, sidecar producer, renamed scanner, Track 1 == Track 2 dishonesty, retained cursor, aux density table, parser-owned structural projection, or second source scan (`restart/prompts/skinny/PASS-2-RESEARCH.md:126`-`131`; `restart/prompts/ORCHESTRATOR.md:81`-`88`). Lock 1 is the controlling rule: tape is the substrate, SIMD masks may be transient producers only, retained structural offsets are the tape, and retained class/mask streams, parser-owned cursor/list state, public substrate APIs, `UnionTape`, or second tapes are rejected without G-Omega amendment (`restart/locks/LOCKS.md:75`, `restart/locks/LOCKS.md:118`-`127`). Lock 1 v+1 also rejects cross-call quote/escape/structural/class/prefix carry (`restart/locks/LOCKS.md:137`-`150`).

## Candidate Disposition Summary

ACCEPT: local byte-set/classifier/movemask operations, tail clamps, UTF-8 validate-only operations, digit-run helpers, same-tape sparse flags/facts, local mask-to-tape writers, and comparator-derived aliases are CH5-safe when they remain `local_temp_only`, `existing_tape`, `direct_sink`, or `admitted_fact_output`.

REVISE: `offset_tape_capacity_policy_v2` must remove the second-source-scan allowance and use only same-loop or existing-tape capacity signals. Direct-cursor and escape/segment surfaces keep their P2-F REVISE status where they need per-grammar template framing, but CH5 has no additional objection as long as no retained cursor, decoded scratch sidecar, or cross-call carry is introduced.

REJECT: any retained `StructuralIndex`, whitespace bitmap, class column, aux density/projection table, decoded-byte sidecar, parser-owned cursor/list, public `UnionTape`, second tape, cross-call classifier carry, PMULL/CSSC production replay, x86 admission route, or Track 2 evidence claimed as Track 1 closure.

## Overall CH5 Verdict

REVISE.

The S-P2 V1 packet preserves Lock 1 in substance: P2-D establishes the tape/projection union, the other P2 files keep masks local or same-tape, and P2-F rejects retained sidecars and diagnostic-only routes. The one CH5 revise is narrow but necessary: V2 must close the `offset_tape_capacity_policy_v2` "second scan" wording so no candidate can be read as authorizing a parallel source pass or capacity sidecar. No current candidate requires CH5 REJECT if that wording is tightened.
