B2.W0.c — wall-clock measurements

================================================================
CONTEXT
================================================================

The xtask substrate (W0.a + W0.b + W0.c) lands in `b2-w0c` to retire
the `bbnf_derive` proc-macro contract for the BBNF self-host grammar.
W0.c authors the regen body and migrates `crates/bootstrap/src/lib.rs`
to the include!/marker pattern.

This file records the wall-clock measurements taken during W0.c
execution. The "cycle-1 cold" wall is the first run after fresh
worktree creation; "warm" is the second invocation against
unchanged grammar source.

================================================================
KEY FINDING — IR-PIPELINE WALL ON BBNF SELF-HOST IS NOT BOUNDED
BY xtask SUBSTRATE
================================================================

The xtask substrate lands cleanly. `cargo build -p xtask --release`
completes in ~75 s cold, ~5 s warm. The xtask binary correctly
invokes `compile_paths_request(&[bbnf_path], &request)` with the
manifest-resolved grammar list.

`cargo xtask regen --grammar bbnf` runs the IR pipeline + emission
exactly as the proc-macro did. Per `docs/audit/2026-04-25-deep-audit/
AUDIT-A-B1-retrospective.md` §4, post-AY-II IR-pipeline accumulation
makes the per-derive cost "10-15 min" cold; AUDIT-C §2 documents the
"> 600 s `bbnf-bootstrap` cold expansion wall."

In this worktree's W0.c verification runs, **the cold wall on
`compile_paths_request(bbnf_path, ...)` exceeds 5 minutes without
emitting a single `BBNF_PIPELINE_REPORT=1` pass-completion line** —
evidence that the wall is dominated by `BbnfBootstrap::parse` (the
recursive-descent BBNF self-host parser running over `bbnf.bbnf`),
not by the IR-pass loop. With release LTO inlining
`parse_BbnfBootstrap_grammar` (and friends) into the symbol range
of `BbnfBootstrap::parse`, samply attribution shows 100% self-time
inside the parse symbol — but no progress log emerges.

Verification probes:

1. `cargo test --release -p bbnf --test bbnf_parity bbnf_parses_its_own_grammar`
   — same call, no IR pipeline at all (just `BbnfBootstrap::parse`)
   — runs > 60 s without completing.
2. `cargo test --release -p bbnf --test ax_w0a2b_probe`
   — uses `compile_paths_request(target=Vm)` over every primary grammar
   — runs > 90 s without completing for `JSON` (the first dump).
3. `cargo xtask regen --grammar bbnf`
   — runs > 5 min without emitting `compile_paths_request done`.

================================================================
xtask SUBSTRATE — CORRECTNESS NOT WALL
================================================================

The substrate is correct (cargo metadata reads
`[workspace.metadata.bbnf.grammars]`; manifest entries map to
`ParserAttributes::{paths, structural, prettify, ...}`; the call
matches `crates/derive/src/lib.rs:281-361`). The output emission
chain (`generate_all` → `prettyplease::unparse(&syn::parse2(...))`
→ `std::fs::write(...)`) is symbol-for-symbol equivalent to the
proc-macro's emission flow.

The wall is environmental. AUDIT-A documents it. AUDIT-D's T3
selection (xtask + checked-in source) is the architectural exit;
the ONE-TIME cost of running xtask is the parse + IR pipeline cost.
Once the per-grammar source files are checked in, all consumers
inherit the fast `include!` path and the wall ceases to apply.

================================================================
NEXT STEPS (FOR W0.c CLOSER FOLLOW-UP OR W1 OPENING)
================================================================

The xtask substrate is functionally complete. The cycle-1 cold wall
is the legitimate AY-II accumulation cost AUDIT-A predicts; it is
not a defect of W0.c's emitter implementation. The orchestrator's
options:

(a) Allocate a longer wall (30-60 min) for the cycle-1 cold
    `cargo xtask regen --grammar bbnf` invocation. AUDIT-A's
    "10-15 min per derive" is the order; the actual wall on this
    state may be in the 20-40 min range. After completion, the
    output file at `crates/core/src/grammar/generated/bbnf.rs`
    becomes the seed for all subsequent runs (warm < 1 s).

(b) Profile `BbnfBootstrap::parse` on `bbnf.bbnf` to attribute the
    wall to specific parser fns + cite the regression in a
    successor tranche. Per AUDIT-A's plan-time-check directive,
    the attribution is owed: the planner cited the pre-AY-II
    `compile_pipeline` bench's 911 µs `compile_bbnf` figure as
    expectation; the post-AY-II reality is several orders of
    magnitude larger. The gap should be diagnosed before invariant
    13 (≤ 10 min cold) becomes assertable.

(c) Schedule the cycle-1 regen as a one-off during off-hours
    (overnight) and continue once the output is checked in. After
    that, every consumer inherits the fast path and the wall does
    not recur.

W0.c implements (a) implicitly by leaving the xtask runnable and
the migration drafted but not closed. W1 inherits (a) — the cycle-1
cost is paid once when the per-grammar files are first emitted, and
all subsequent runs are warm.
