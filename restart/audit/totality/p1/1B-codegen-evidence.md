## Executive Summary

The skinny codegen layer continues to expose the named five-shape `BackendShape` enum, the `LayoutFacts.backend_shape` + `LayoutFacts.cost_facts` side tables, and the `derive_backend_shape_with_diagnostics` plan builder. V6 finds three load-bearing material changes since V4/V5: (1) `CostFacts` is no longer a thin priority/rationale record — `active_cost: Option<ActiveCostFacts>` and `decision_csp: Option<DecisionCspFacts>` carry rich e-graph and CSP solver evidence vectors (selection trace hash, frontier candidate counts, CSP solve micros, scalarization status, replay determinism); (2) ARCH §7.4 now explicitly retires the `shapes_for_json`/`nominate_json`/`materialization_for_rule` stale-prose claim (`restart/ARCHITECTURE.md:1149-1154`); (3) `lower::rust::lower_to_rust` is now W7 fail-closed at `skinny/crates/codegen/src/lower/rust.rs:27-77` — emission refuses without active-cost and SAT decision-CSP facts.

The remaining V1 codegen gaps stand: the formal `Backend` two-method trait at ARCH §7.6 is absent (only `ShapeLowering::lower_rule` exists); four of five shape lowerers still emit marker strings (`rule {} -> <shape>`); per-grammar runtime profile selection is hardcoded across 8 providers under `RuntimeProvider::{Json, CssL4DeclarationValues, CssL4DeclarationValuesExtended, CssL4StylesheetSelectors, CssL4VisualFunctions, CssL4AtRulesAndMedia, CssL4VendorAndCustomAtRules, CssL4NestedLayout}` (`skinny/crates/codegen/src/grammar_profile.rs:17-26`); recognizer mining and materialization-role mining remain JSON-shape-biased. SK-V14 binding: the 8-provider count IS the Lock 14 recurrence vector — no css_pretty provider exists in codegen/ (consistent with v3 audit pack); the css_pretty Pattern H module lives in `crates/core/src/runtime/css_pretty/` only.
