# BB-D.W6 — visual, interaction, accessibility, and performance proof

## Goal

Prove the current packed frontend as a deliberate, responsive, accessible product
through two design passes and two clean visual audits.

## Required two-pass design plan

Before component edits, record:

1. **Foundation pass:** semantic color roles, typography hierarchy, spacing rhythm,
   radii/borders/shadows, motion timing/easing, surface depth, breakpoints, focus,
   reduced motion, and data/status encodings. Reuse Glass tokens where their
   semantics match; introduce no arbitrary one-off tokens.
2. **Composition pass:** app/landing/docs/playground layouts, information hierarchy,
   editor/result/stage panels, controls, empty/loading/error states, responsive
   transformations, and keyboard/focus order. Apply tokens consistently.

## Verification loop

1. Re-extract the complete route and docs-template inventory; create one assay row
   for every page, including redirects and failure/deep-link states. Use the
   `frontend-design` skill for each page's baseline diagnosis and each redesign
   review, not only for the shared shell.
2. Build/load the exact packed/provenanced Glass 7.0.0 and sibling artifacts in the
   in-app browser.
3. Capture desktop and mobile screenshots for every route and important state;
   capture interactions, keyboard/focus order, accessibility tree/contrast,
   console/network logs, layout shift, bundle chunks, and runtime profiles.
4. Critique screenshots against hierarchy, density, alignment, rhythm, readability,
   distinctiveness, overflow, and state legibility. Correct at the owning token or
   component abstraction, then repeat.
5. Run two fresh hostile visual/interaction reviews and adjudicate every finding.
6. Require two consecutive complete clean visual audits over the entire inventory;
   a newly discovered page/state invalidates a partial clean pass.

## Gates

- No console/network error, overflow, clipped/unreachable control, keyboard trap,
  focus loss, contrast/accessibility failure, stale artifact, or unexplained layout
  shift/regression.
- Mobile is a designed transformation, not compressed desktop.
- Every inventoried page has its own light/dark, 1440x1000, 1024x768, 390x844, and
  320x700 disposition, with explicit state exceptions rather than inferred parity.
- Bundle/runtime regressions have raw evidence and correction or adjudicated reason.
- Screenshots and traces identify exact commit/artifact/browser viewport.

## Completion

BB-D closes with a visually and behaviorally proven packed product.
