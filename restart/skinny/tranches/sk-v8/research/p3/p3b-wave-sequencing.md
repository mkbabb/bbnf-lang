S-P2 is converged and only authorizes S-P3 planning. The V7 consolidation records 6/6 ACCEPT with no REVISE/REJECT (`S-P2 V7 consolidated verdict and preserved boundaries`) and says V6 plus V7 form the required two consecutive qualifying ACCEPT cycles (`S-P2 V7 consolidated verdict and preserved boundaries`). It does not authorize W3 redress, any implementation wave, or G-Alpha close by itself (`S-P2 V7 consolidated verdict and preserved boundaries`).

S-P3 is a planning pass, not an execution pass. Its prompt is read-only against `skinny/` source (`PASS-3 Synthesis-Plan role and gate sections`), and P3-B specifically orders the shortlist into waves with W0 as baseline/telemetry and behavior waves after it (`PASS-3 Synthesis-Plan role and gate sections`). W0 is therefore first and blocks every behavior wave until it closes (`PASS-3 Synthesis-Plan role and gate sections`).

The current measured surface remains observability-bound: `RESULTS schema-v3 current main rows and Track 2 authority` reports `Strictness=deferred`, `parse_utf8=view-boundary`, placeholder hot leaves, and no machine-readable SK-V6 delta across current rows (`RESULTS schema-v3 current main rows and Track 2 authority`). The handoff summarizes the same state as 17 `parse_only` K/NO-GO rows, 11 `direct_to_struct` N-direct/NO-GO rows, 6 direct GO rows, and 4 real typed GO rows (`HANDOFF Sections 2, 3a, and 4-10`). Because strict admission requires matching output plane, `comparator_strictness=strict`, same-run freshness or native strict anchors, and in-row UTF-8/control/escape validation (`SPEC Sections 0.1-0.5, 2, and 3-11`), no behavior wave may use current deferred parse rows as strict SOTA admission evidence.

The P3-B sequence is:

1. **W0 - Baseline Profile And Telemetry Lock.** Create `SK-V8-open`; bind required telemetry, run ids, profile artifacts, sidecar freshness, comparator fields, CostFacts placeholders, and the Lock 14 baseline allowlist before behavior work.
2. **W1 - CostFacts Gate Binding.** After W0, make CostFacts evidence executable in `gate-json` so route-quality claims have a gate before W2/W3/W4 can admit.
3. **W2 - Typed Product Plane Expansion.** After W0/W1 and a plan update, preserve the four current real typed GO rows and extend only generated typed product rows backed by explicit host/API facts and an independent Track 2 or oracle.
4. **W3 - Profile-Selected Parse Candidate.** After W0/W1, fresh W3 plan, and challenge, the lead candidate is Tier A only: tape plus structural-projection union as structural-class cursor migration. It is not a Tier B string-boundary/parity/CostFacts-template union, not `tape_vs_tape`, and not an automatic implementation dispatch.
5. **W4 - Direct Guard Triage.** After W0/W1 and after W2/W3 disposition or explicit route, triage selected direct guard rows without presenting digest stressors as product-plane SOTA proof.
6. **W5 - Grammar-Neutral Audit And Lock 14 Preservation.** After W1-W4 are admitted, rejected, or routed, run the close audit against generic-crate JSON policy leaks and non-JSON proof obligations.
7. **W6 - Close And Alpha Feedback.** Reconcile wave dispositions, `RESULTS schema-v3 current main rows and Track 2 authority`, `REDRESS entries named in this sentence or table row`, `HANDOFF Sections 2, 3a, and 4-10`, SK-V9 residuals, and Pass Omega residuals.

The pre-P3 SK-V8 SPEC priced W0-W6 at 120-300 minute wave hard caps. This P3-B plan applies the stricter current dispatch rule: every implementation/redress wave is capped at **90 minutes maximum**, inclusive of source implementation, focused verification, generated-output review, gate refresh within the wave budget, `RESULTS schema-v3 current main rows and Track 2 authority`, `REDRESS entries named in this sentence or table row`, and close documentation. If a wave cannot fit that cap, the plan must split the wave before redress or mark the route blocked/revised. The generic orchestrator requires explicit minute caps and halting at the cap (`PASS-3 Synthesis-Plan role and gate sections`).

### W3 lead candidate boundary

The same-wave production consumer is the retained JSON parser path consuming retained `Tape` positions/classes (`SC-2 Tier A/Tier B split and strict threshold provenance`).

Tier B remains blocked from W3 by default. It owns string-boundary facts, quote/backslash/parity masks, string-boundary consumers, and CostFacts-template parity (`SC-2 Tier A/Tier B split and strict threshold provenance`; `SC-4 string-plane gap and Tier B reserve`). It requires its own future plan, strict rows, owner paths, cap, same-wave consumer, and challenge.

SC-5 says it is W0/W1-plan telemetry, not W3 production consumer or current SOTA admission evidence (`SC-5 parse_only demotion and tape_vs_tape limits`), and the SPEC explicitly says it cannot satisfy W3's production same-wave consumer (`SPEC Sections 0.1-0.5, 2, and 3-11`).
