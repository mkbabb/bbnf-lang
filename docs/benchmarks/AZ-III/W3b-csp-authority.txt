Architectural notes
-------------------

1. Each installer reads upstream FACTS (recognizer.shape,
   delim_scan_configs, key_dispatch_configs, keyword_branches) and
   issues `Csp::add_equals(var, value)` — the canonical pin
   primitive owned by the csp-solver crate. There is no inline
   `LambdaConstraint::new` boilerplate in any installer (W3b.4
   alignment).

2. `ConstraintCtx` carries four per-component variable maps:
   `engine_vars` (existing), `alt_vars` and `wrap_vars` (added
   AZ-III.W3b). Each installer projects from the relevant map.

3. The grammar-wide shape-dict admission selection
   (`solve_shape_dict_selection`) remains a separate pass because
   the budget cardinality decision is not per-component. Per the
   updated module docstring, the per-component `shape_dict::install`
   no-op was deleted; per-site shape authority lives in
   `shape::install`.
