## §1 — Synthesis

The shortlist is seven candidate waves, under the requested cap of eight. It keeps the accepted Alpha wave shape but narrows the behavior candidates to the converged S-P2 boundaries: W0 telemetry first, W1 CostFacts before behavior, provisional W2 typed product work, W3 Tier A tape plus structural-projection union, W4 direct guard triage, W5 Lock 14 audit, and W6 close. Existing SPEC caps above 90 minutes are not carried as implementation authority here; each redress/implementation slice must be planned at <=90 minutes, or P3-B/P3-F must split the wave before dispatch.
