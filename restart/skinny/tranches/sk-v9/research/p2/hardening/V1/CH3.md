## §1 — Method (REDRESS cross-check)

CH3's binding question is whether any proposed intervention in S-P2 V1
re-opens a route already rejected in `skinny/REDRESS.md`, whether the
HANDOFF §5 pre-block list is correctly identified, and whether any admitted
row (4 typed-GO `real_typed_struct` + 3 direct-GO + the 13 `parse_only`
guards in the SK-V9-open report at `skinny/RESULTS.md`) is at risk of silent
regression.

The procedure on each report:

1. Enumerate every intervention claim that touches a route prior REDRESS
   entries name (substrate/sidecar, parser-owned cursor, retained-parse
   helper rewire, ASM hot-body fold, codec-into-materialiser wiring,
   row-baseline gate flip).
2. Verify the report cites the relevant REDRESS entry by number AND states
   a *material* differential — not a cosmetic relabel.
3. Cross-check the differential against the cited entry's verbatim
   rejection text in `skinny/REDRESS.md` and the HANDOFF §5 pre-block
   list. A differential is *material* iff it changes one of: (a) the
   producer (who writes), (b) the consumer (who reads), (c) the
   wave-class (parse_only vs direct vs typed vs gate-only), or (d) the
   measured shape (call-site, batching width, µop count).
4. For ASM/SIMD proposals, additionally verify the SK-V7 A3 five-invariant
   gate (scalar oracle, forced feature masks, ABI-checked-call shim,
   recoverable fault, cycle counter) is respected at S-P2 depth.
5. Verify no admitted row's *substrate*, *codegen path*, or *gate boundary*
   silently changes shape under the proposal — even where the proposal is
   nominally on a different row, a shared owner-file edit can move the row
   indirectly.

§3W CH3 enforces the §5 HANDOFF list verbatim: "Apache/CITM measured-row
overclaim from REDRESS 91", "W3 structural implementation without retained
class/event grammar plus retained `ValueRef` cursor proof", "W4 scalar-parent
fold or renamed parent-digest fold without a V9-aware checked gate",
"Sidecar substrate, parser-owned cursor/fact slots, `UnionTape`, new
`BackendShape`, new directive/BIR, public substrate API, `tape_vs_tape` as
production consumer", "PMULL prefix-XOR and CTZ/bulk production rewires as
default hot paths", and "Generic JSON policy leaks or Lock 14 weakening".

## §2 — Disposition table per report

Each row is one REDRESS-adjacent claim made by the report. The columns are:
*REDRESS entry cited* (the entry the report names), *Claim* (the proposed
move), *Differential stated* (the report's argument that the route is
materially different), *CH3 verdict* (ACCEPT / REVISE / REJECT). REVISE means
the differential is correct in principle but the report's framing or the
remaining surface area is incomplete; the fold into V2 is named in §4.
