## Governing Criteria

CH2 asks whether every candidate carries a P2-F grammar-neutral verdict; JSON-only primitives with no byte-set, classifier, tape, or per-grammar-template expression are REVISE or REJECT; candidates must work for CSS L4 / Sheets / BBNF-self or be reframed as per-grammar template surfaces (`restart/prompts/skinny/PASS-2-RESEARCH.md:102`-`107`). The universal CH2 lens is stricter about grammar-name leakage: Lock 14 must hold, with no grammar-name leak and no JSON-only intervention (`restart/prompts/ORCHESTRATOR.md:74`-`85`).

Lock 14 forbids generic crates from carrying grammar-specific branches, grammar-named modules, grammar-specific public API types, or per-grammar feature flags (`restart/locks/LOCKS.md:349`-`356`). It also requires generated/provider policy ownership and grammar-shape gates before fleet-wide transfer claims (`restart/locks/LOCKS.md:377`-`390`), and shared `bbnf-simd` / parse-that APIs may expose only grammar-neutral facts and primitives (`restart/locks/LOCKS.md:392`-`400`).
