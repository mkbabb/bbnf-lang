# BB-A.W5 — first structure apotheosis

## Authority

Closes BB-A after W0–W4 are GREEN. This is an iterative graph-to-code loop, not a
single review meeting.

## Dynamic partition

The extractor partitions by dependency SCC, articulation point, fan-in/out,
ownership boundary, generated lineage, test reachability, package face, and
frontend feature. A shard targets roughly 24 nodes or 80 internal edges; larger
clusters are recursively split with a boundary quotient and cycle basis. Smaller
clusters merge only when their invariant and reviewers are shared.

## Loop

1. Emit the complete shard, all internal/boundary edges, source line anchors,
   metrics, cycle basis, and owner/disposition evidence.
2. Dispatch two fresh explicit-Sol critics that assume every node/edge/grouping is
   wrong. Neither sees a favored design.
3. Dispatch a fresh explicit-Sol adjudicator to reproduce each claim and rule it
   correct, incorrect, or unproven.
4. Implement every correct finding at the owning abstraction. Re-extract the whole
   graph; reopen both incident shards for any changed edge.
5. Evaluate Goldilocks structure: cohesive invariant, narrow surface, readable
   local control flow, no god module, no sand wrappers, no parent-name stutter, no
   cross-owner cycle, and no test/provenance concealment.
6. Continue until two consecutive complete post-correction audits are clean.

## Evidence gates

- Every node and edge is covered by all three views; coverage is computed from
  emitted identities, not a prose checklist.
- No nontrivial shipping dependency SCC crosses an ownership boundary.
- Unsupported/unknown extraction facts keep their shard RED.
- Every accepted move reduces or justifies complexity with behavior unchanged.
- Two complete clean audits have verified model metadata and immutable hashes.

## Completion

BB-A closes with a canonical product plane, complete topology, external tests,
provenanced artifacts, Apple-only policy, and a directed shell ready for semantic
stage replacement. Any structural edge change reopens its incident shards.
