## Triumvirate Dispatch

Dispatch a triumvirate if any of these occurs:

- a rewrite rule can be admitted only through a test-only path or without VM-residue/ranker evidence;
- CSP authority cannot be made consumer-authoritative without creating a second decision system;
- regex scanner activation needs a parallel hand-written parser;
- shape_dict or structural scan needs a new runtime substrate not already present;
- DTA cleanup reveals a current walker/tape dependency.
- denominator enumeration reveals more active substrates than W4 file bounds can own;
- WIRED-NOT-CONSUMED count (from substrate matrix in `audit/HARDENING-2026-05-01-babbage.md`) exceeds 5 at audit start, OR CONSUMED count is less than 60% of total substrates audited at W4 mid-point;
- three diagnostic-loop iterations in any sub-unit fail to isolate root cause.

Empty/no-evidence returns get one verbatim redispatch with the same worktree pointer. A second empty/no-evidence return triggers mandatory triumvirate. Three diagnostic-loop iterations without isolating root cause auto-trigger triumvirate. JSONL transcript quiet >15 minutes is an orchestrator-side auto-trigger; record the condition and dispatch time in `PROGRESS.md` alongside the wave's evidence ledger.

## Disjointness

Rewrite/ruler, CSP/regex, DTA/dfa cleanup, shape/SIMD, and Pratt/view units have separate primary files. Any shared pipeline file change is owned by AZ-IV.W4.1 and reviewed before other W4 integration. The denominator ledger is shared evidence, not a shared write surface; each unit writes its owned section.

Per `audit/HARDENING-2026-05-01-cantor.md` Amendment 2: W4.2 owns CSP regex/strategy/dispatch leaves (`crates/ir/src/passes/csp_strategy/strategy.rs`, `regex.rs`); W4.3 owns CSP shape/scan leaves (`shape.rs`, `structural_scan.rs`) and `shapes/{dispatcher,inline,array}/**`; W4.5 owns `shapes/pratt/**` and view rendering. The `csp_strategy/**` and `shapes/**` globs are split per-leaf, never co-modified.

W4 also touches several files reserved by W0, W1, W2, or W3 in earlier waves (`xtask/src/regen.rs` after W0, `crates/ir/src/egraph/**` after W0, `crates/core/src/backend/rust/emitter/shapes/**` after W1). Sequencing guarantees disjointness across the wave boundary; W4 may not open until W3 closes and HEAD is clean for those paths. The cross-wave doc paths (`docs/codegen-paths.md`, etc.) are explicitly modify-carve in W4 so the W0-modify ownership remains primary.
