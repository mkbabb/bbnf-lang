## §1 — Synthesis

SK-V12's pre-block ledger has one load-bearing ordering rule: the generated
non-JSON baseline comes first. `SYNTHESIS.md` requires a generated Track 1
non-JSON direct or typed parser, independent Track 2 or oracle, strict equality,
W1-thresholded same-run throughput, provenance, run/build telemetry, and
gate/report consumption before any JSON-only micro-wave dispatches
(`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v12/SYNTHESIS.md:38`-`:49`). `HANDOFF.md` says the
same priority is binding and names CSS L4 declaration values, Sheets, then
BBNF-self as the preferred fallback order
(`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v12/HANDOFF.md:60`-`:70`).

The current result surface is unchanged from SK-V11 close: 16 parse rows are
`S / NO-GO`, `canada/parse_only` is `L / NO-GO`, direct has 4 `A / GO` and 13
`N-direct / NO-GO`, typed has 7 `A / GO`, and overall remains
`N-direct / NoGo` (`skinny/RESULTS.md:143`-`:146`;
`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v12/HANDOFF.md:41`-`:47`). REDRESS 120 is the seed
close authority: SK-V11 made no behavior source, generated runtime, benchmark
body, gate semantic, or `RESULTS.md` movement, and it routes SK-V12 to solve the
generated non-JSON baseline before revisiting direct residuals
(`skinny/REDRESS.md:3531`-`:3553`).

The S-P2 candidate pool is therefore admissible only through grammar-neutral
generated consumers. P2-A says comparator candidates must stay grammar-neutral,
preserve the single substrate, carry scalar references, and be consumed
immediately by generated parser/product paths
(`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v12/research/p2/p2a-sota-teardown.md:23`-`:37`).
P2-B makes scalar oracle, strict checkasm, caller micro-proof, and same-wave
consumer the process floor for ASM/SIMD (`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v12/research/p2/p2b-dav1d-process.md:27`-`:35`).
P2-C keeps AArch64-only candidates in scope and demotes LD4/SHA3 ideas to
inventory until fresh P1 evidence names the missing stream/expression
(`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v12/research/p2/p2c-arch-esoterica.md:34`-`:42`,
`:122`-`:133`). P2-D contributes no selectable substrate candidate and rejects
the structural class-lane union outright (`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v12/research/p2/p2d-substrate-tape.md:69`-`:83`).
P2-E lists parse-that primitives only as gaps with same-wave generated
consumers, not as JSON direct authority (`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v12/research/p2/p2e-parse-that-gaps.md:46`-`:47`,
`:374`-`:394`). P2-F says the generator is still JSON-profiled at runtime
emission and that Lock 14 requires generated per-grammar modules, not generic
crate grammar policy (`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v12/research/p2/p2f-grammar-neutral.md:14`-`:19`).

The ledger conclusion is not "all primitive work is blocked." It is narrower:
primitive work may admit only when a wave gives it a scalar/oracle reference,
strict parity or equivalent equality, a same-wave generated consumer, a
measurable row gate, and a material differential from the REDRESS route it
touches. Otherwise the route is a pre-blocked retry.

## §4 — Pre-blocked routes

Hard pre-blocks no SK-V12 wave may reopen under the current contract:

1. W3 union/event/class-column/streaming-cursor/class-lane/sidecar substrate,
   including `UnionTape`, retained structural vectors, parser-owned projections,
   and W4-through-W3 cascade-lock.
2. Parse-only SOTA close, parse-only row admission, or parse-only evidence as a
   generated non-JSON or direct product proof.
3. JSON direct residual row movement before the generated non-JSON baseline and
   selected-baseline intervention priority succeeds or records a measured block.
4. JSON direct residual row movement after that priority unless fresh evidence
   exceeds REDRESS 114-119 with a material source delta, scalar/oracle proof,
   same-host microbench, independent Track 2, strict sonic direct floor, and
   same-wave gate consumption.
5. REDRESS 111 report/gate lane treated as generated Track 1 baseline.
6. W0-clamped row admission, docs-only direct admission, or row movement by
   table arithmetic.
7. Direct digest evidence as typed product proof, or output digest/hash as a
   parser primitive without a legal row-owned product.
8. Admission by analogy from another row or output plane.
9. Replays of `number_span_emit_slot`, `container_tail_next`, bounded string
   span, decoded-byte source folds, output-digest host-sink, StringBlock16,
   single-quartet/x4 proof-only, PMULL prefix-XOR default body, or CSSC CTZ bulk
   consumer without material differential and new row/oracle evidence.
10. PMU, cycles, structural-scan, masking probes, Criterion slope, sidecar
    freshness, or parser inventory as behavior producers.
11. JSON policy in generic crates or runtime outside generated per-grammar code.
12. New directive, BIR variant, `BackendShape`, public substrate API,
    parser-owned sidecar/fact slot, parser-owned structural projection,
    retained structural cursor or cursor list, aux density table, aux projection
    column, event side vector, whitespace bitmap, retained class lane,
    structural-position vector, decoded-byte sidecar, renamed retained scanner,
    or second retained substrate.
13. x86 implementation work.

Routes that may admit only under different framing with fresh P1 evidence:

- Same-tape capacity, sparse-flag, or retained cursor-skip diagnostics may enter
  a later shortlist only if a fresh profile names builder capacity, flag lookup,
  or retained traversal as a hot leaf and the wave keeps the single tape
  substrate (`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v12/research/p2/p2d-substrate-tape.md:46`-`:51`,
  `:109`-`:111`).
- LD4 interleaved classification may enter only if a fresh profile names an
  existing canonical interleaved stream and the wave supplies scalar
  deinterleave oracle, checkasm parity, and same-loop generated consumer
  (`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v12/research/p2/p2c-arch-esoterica.md:55`-`:65`).
- SHA3 ternary boolean fold may enter only if a fresh profile names the exact
  hot three-input expression and the wave supplies scalar formula, checkasm
  parity, and generated/runtime consumer
  (`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v12/research/p2/p2c-arch-esoterica.md:122`-`:133`).
- A JSON direct companion may enter only after non-JSON priority resolves and
  the wave clears the full REDRESS 114-119 reopen burden.

Typed-output boundary: REDRESS 70/71 stay binding for W1 typed-equivalent
baselines. A typed baseline may not use a hand-authored typed sink, direct
digest proof, hidden directive/BIR extension, hidden host schema, or
benchmark-private Track 1 parser. It admits only through generated DirectBuild
or schema-source facts, independent oracle equality, provenance, and same-wave
gate consumption.
