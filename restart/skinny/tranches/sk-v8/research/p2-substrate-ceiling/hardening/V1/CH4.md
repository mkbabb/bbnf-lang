## §5 — Verdict Rationale

V1 has the right target but over-compresses the cost. A narrow W3 substrate-consumption migration can be defended: it has a plausible hot parser consumer, a stated scalar/checkasm obligation, and a source cap path inside SPEC §2 if the owner list stays tight. The full string-plane closure cannot be sold as that same W3 slice. SC-4's quote/backslash/parity index, CostFacts/template work, and multi-wave warning make the larger claim too expensive for W3 as written.

`tape_vs_tape` is also not free cover for substrate admission. It is useful gate work, probably W1-shaped, but its consumer is the benchmark table, not the production parser. V2 must separate telemetry/gate consumers from production same-wave consumers, price the Pass Omega fork explicitly, and stop hiding multi-wave or Omega dependency under the W3 label.
