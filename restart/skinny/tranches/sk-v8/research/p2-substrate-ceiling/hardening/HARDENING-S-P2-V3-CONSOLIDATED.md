### 1. Strict Comparator Evidence Still Drifts

The V3 fold still violates strict-vs-strict comparator discipline in load-bearing
research prose. `skinny/RESULTS.md:3` defines the post-row delta order as
`Delta vs SK-V6`, `Delta vs sonic-strict`, `Delta vs simdjson DOM`, and
`Delta vs yyjson`. Under that order, canada is `+27.9%` vs sonic-strict and
`+54.6%` vs simdjson DOM (`skinny/RESULTS.md:10`), mesh is `+21.4%` vs
sonic-strict and `+51.5%` vs simdjson DOM (`skinny/RESULTS.md:19`), and
citm_catalog is `+24.6%` vs sonic-strict and `-11.3%` vs simdjson DOM
(`skinny/RESULTS.md:8`). Sidecar provenance is explicitly non-admission policy
(`skinny/RESULTS.md:219`).

SC-4 and SC-5 now state that correctly: SC-4 separates canada/mesh strict values
from simdjson sidecar values (`SC-4-string-plane-gap.md:93-105`), and SC-5
states the authoritative taxonomy (`SC-5-k-classification-adjudication.md:39-61`).
But SC-1 still labels canada `+54.6%`, mesh `+51.5%`, citm_catalog `-11.3%`,
update_center `-63.4%`, apache_builds `-65.3%`, github_events `-61.7%`,
distinct_values `-70.8%`, and y_string_unicode `-54.4%` as same-run sonic-strict
or strict evidence (`SC-1-offset-tape-teardown.md:101-116`,
`SC-1-offset-tape-teardown.md:196-199`). SC-2 repeats canada `+54.6% vs sonic
strict`, mesh `+51.5%`, twitter `-35.8%`, update_center `-63.4%`,
distinct_values `-70.8%`, and apache_builds `-65.3%` in its string-density
argument (`SC-2-two-stage-sota.md:248-253`). SC-3 repeats the shifted
canada/mesh magnitudes as the union-substrate throughput basis
(`SC-3-union-substrate-design.md:75-82`), and SYNTHESIS still says the
highest-ratio canada/mesh/marine_ik corpora are the rows where bbnf wins by
"roughly +50%" (`SYNTHESIS.md:97-101`).

This blocks V3 because a future planner could copy SC-1/SC-2/SC-3/SYNTHESIS
thresholds while believing they are strict sonic evidence. SPEC's refusal rule
already requires matching comparator plane, strictness, same-run freshness, and
measured-row validation (`SPEC.md:117-123`); the research packet must not carry
contradictory magnitudes beside that rule. The strict-vs-strict comparator
