## Section 1 - Corrected Diagnosis

SK-V10 preserved the typed product win and moved one additional direct row,
but it did not close the direct plane. W0's fresh SK-V11-open run shows the
current JSON close pressure is not parse-only throughput; it is the 13 direct
digest residual rows, three of which are W0-clamped planning rows rather than
behavior admissions.

The W3 falsification is now a hard route boundary, not a planning caveat.
REDRESS 98 states that two faithful union-substrate implementations made the
claim measurable and both regressed uniformly (`skinny/REDRESS.md:2910`).
The scalar delimiter path is cheaper than the retained SIMD structural cursor
on this host (`skinny/REDRESS.md:2928`).

The W7/W8/W9 sequence is the second boundary. A primitive can pass scalar and
checkasm parity and still fail caller-level throughput (`skinny/REDRESS.md:3159`).
A primitive can pass caller microbench and still fail production if the caller
is already wired or row floors fail (`skinny/REDRESS.md:3200`). SK-V11's
micro-prove-first rule must therefore include the production consumer and row
gate, not just isolated assembly throughput.

The unexercised thesis risk is grammar generalization. SK-V10 close routes
CSS L4, Sheets, and BBNF-self risk to the totality track
(`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v10/research/close/close-redress.md:71`). SK-V11
pulls one such proof into skinny close because four JSON-only skinny cycles
are not enough to prove the generator thesis.
