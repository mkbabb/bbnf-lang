# SK-V25 lock constitution

The old registries are evidence banks, not verdict authority. This constitution
defines the new decision surfaces, their proof obligations, and the order in which
they may converge.

## One design loop

Every lock and every graph cluster uses the same six-step loop:

1. **Census** — reproduce behavior, enumerate consumers, and capture the current
   typed multigraph. No design verdict is allowed.
2. **Design** — produce at least four materially distinct families. State the
   family withheld from each researcher so convergence cannot be prompted.
3. **Prototype** — run the smallest artifact that can kill the leading claim.
   Compile-only work is not runtime proof; passing existing tests is not novelty.
4. **Apotheosis** — two fresh Sol critics independently assume the design and its
   graph are wrong. A third fresh Sol adjudicator labels each finding correct,
   incorrect, or unproven against rerunnable evidence.
5. **Transposition** — supersede the design, implement the proved carrier, delete
   losing paths, and recapture graph and performance deltas.
6. **Clean audits** — two consecutive fresh audits find no unresolved semantic,
   structural, package, performance, or ownership defect. Any finding resets the
   count and reopens step 2 at the affected cluster.

An agent may not criticise or adjudicate its own design. Every spawn declares
`gpt-5.6-sol`; recorded `turn_context.model` is checked at each workflow boundary.

## Implemented-wave acceptance law

No implemented or “verified” wave is accepted from author tests, receipts,
benchmarks, or a single apotheosis. Henceforward every implementation must survive
at least two fresh hostile gestalt passes. Each pass examines all four planes:

1. **tranche fit** — whether this wave was the right experiment and remains the
   smallest useful route through the full campaign;
2. **wave fidelity** — every specification clause, deliberate deviation, friction,
   casualty, and unimplemented remainder;
3. **feature truth** — semantics, failure behavior, resources, public surface,
   structure, package/artifact identity, and measured cost for every feature;
4. **greater-product effect** — new or removed graph edges, ownership, duplication,
   legacy survival, downstream fit, and whether the wave creates a god module,
   sand, a second authority, or a needless mechanism.

Both passes use fresh Sol critics who assume the wave is wrong and a fresh
adjudicator who reproduces or disproves every finding. The author supplies only an
immutable source/command ledger plus a concise friction and deviation note; it
cannot score itself. A correct finding immediately reopens the wave, is fixed in
the owning implementation, and resets the consecutive-clean count. Acceptance
requires two consecutive clean passes over the corrected implementation.

Parsimony is load-bearing: reviewers compare the candidate against a simpler
control and identify removable lines, modules, state, indirection, gates, and
artifacts. Process exists only where it proves a product invariant. Direct code,
external source-isomorphic tests, executable counterexamples, and visual/runtime
proof take priority over additional registries or prose.

## Complete-candidate credit law

No partial carrier may be called, counted, ranked, or reported as a product
prototype. Every credited candidate independently implements every PT and BB lock,
the complete hybrid value/tape API, the entire frozen JSON and CSS Level 4 scope,
exact Rust and TypeScript types inferred solely from BBNF module bytes, all required
Rust/TypeScript/WASM/Apple-ARM surfaces, packages, consumers, resources, profiles,
mutations, and both clean hostile gestalt passes. A narrow parser, regex, type,
graph, JSON, CSS, tape, backend, or assembly artifact is an `INCOMPLETE_ASSAY` even
when its local tests pass. Sidecar schemas, hidden type tables, handwritten semantic
manifests, shims, aliases, fallbacks, shared candidate implementation, and
configuration-only variants earn zero credit. The four VT implementations are
independent and orthogonal; all four must close completely and performantly.

Every original family `1A` through `9D` receives executable treatment. Mutually
exclusive families need not coexist in one product, but none may disappear by
renaming: it must either be implemented inside a complete VT candidate or be
refuted/dominated by an independently runnable assay that preserves the full
observable contract and names the lost capability and measured cost. Prose,
consumer count, architectural taste, or another candidate's implementation cannot
retire a family.

The eight historical cross-lock rows are also born RED: XL-A routes to the
structural graph and B gestalts; XL-B to BB-2/BB-3; XL-C to PT-R/BB-3/BB-7 and

PT-V and the fresh Value snapshot; and XL-H to every source/profile receipt. Each
must be proved, disproved, or superseded from current bytes rather than inherited.

Each VT candidate emits its own `evidence/lock-coverage.tsv`. It has one atomic row
for every current born-RED bullet, each historical lock task and family, XL-A
through XL-H, and every named cut, owner gate, experiment, audit, gestalt, and
letter obligation in `INVENTORY.md` section D. Required columns are `obligation`,
`source_digest`, `owner`, `implementation`, `test`, `mutation`, `profile`,
`artifact`, `critic_a`, `critic_b`, `adjudication`, and `disposition`. Paths and
digests—not words such as “covered” or “architectural”—populate evidence fields.
One aggregate lock row, a missing path, shared candidate evidence, or an
`UNMAPPED`, `UNRUN`, or `UNPROVEN` terminal row keeps that candidate RED.

## Cross-control dominance law

Semantic totality precedes speed. The selected production architecture must
implement every PT/BB lock and reach zero discrepancy over the entire frozen JSON
and CSS Level 4 scope before a control ratio can count. No excluded Level-4 row,
unsupported feature, parser-only surface, disabled diagnostic, missing value/type
operation, reduced serializer, or omitted crossing may be exchanged for speed.

VT.W0 freezes the fastest correct Apple/aarch64 artifact among the latest stable
release and the then-current upstream default-branch revision for each control,
including exact source, toolchain, features, build flags, adapter, corpus, and
operation. The 2026-07-19 discovery anchors are Lightning CSS `v1.32.0` at
`7f8a861bdee476fe90c89a8badeb3fd33a99c51a` with upstream `master` at
`91bfa3fcde39e03ce33a83475f6c55e3c3e50dd2`, and sonic-rs `v0.5.8` dereferenced
to `e339451e37a8f2d3f9b93f155985d31fcbcb115a` with upstream `main` at
`03545a9530346fe279b674dd496e037d94204bc5`. These are inspection anchors, not
floating authority; the freeze must refresh them and choose the faster correct
build without weakening its native path.

For predeclared matched operations and every mandatory corpus stratum, the lower
bound of the paired bootstrap 95% confidence interval must show:

- at least **5.0x** speedup over the best correct Lightning CSS control for CSS
  parse and parse/transform/serialize pipelines, with **6.0x** the target; and
- at least **1.1x** speedup over the best correct sonic-rs control for matched JSON
  parse/value, query/navigation, and serialization pipelines, with **2.0x** the
  target.

Unsupported or incorrect control cases remain correctness facts and never become
infinite speedups. A composite score, selected favorable file, warm-only run,
microkernel, or less-complete observation surface cannot satisfy either ratio.
Every complete VT candidate is measured against both targets; losing candidates
may receive BANK/RETIRE only after remaining fully implemented, while no candidate
may receive ADVANCE and the product may not transpose if neither target is met.

## Generality and perfected-union law

The product is one grammar-driven system, not a JSON/CSS/benchmark special case.
JSON is an early end-to-end falsification carrier and cannot select a production
architecture alone. Every production-facing mechanism must derive from grammar,
semantic HIR, typed host capabilities, target capabilities, and an explicit cost
policy; branching on fixture/rule/grammar names is forbidden.

The union has one-home ownership:

- BBNF owns the modular language, modules/namespaces, decorators, canonical
  grammar/program HIR, type/effect/value schema, analysis facts, backend-neutral
  lowering plan, cost problem, and generated-artifact provenance;
- parse-that owns the TypeScript and Rust parser execution kernels, combinators,
  source/coordinate contract, scanner fabric, canonical regex algebra/HIR and
  automata engines;
- regex syntax is itself BBNF and lowers directly into the canonical regex HIR.
  No handwritten production parser, shadow schema, sidecar markup language, or
  second HIR may survive bootstrap closure;
- Rust and TypeScript expose isomorphic semantic/result/type/module boundaries.
  Low-level Rust-only implementations are explicit one-home kernels with shared
  fixtures, not empty TypeScript mirrors;
- BBNF and parse-that share one versioned multidimensional cost vocabulary and
  calibration corpus. They may have separate solvers only during comparison; the
  selected plan and cost receipt have one authority.

The generalized type/value API must derive from BBNF alone and cover open/closed
records and variants, recursive binders, tuples/lists/options/functions, effects,
host capabilities, source-qualified spans, units/dimensions, CSS/L4 token/value
families, recovery products, and backend layout projections. A missing type is a
typed error; neither `Span` nor an opaque value is a default.

Optimization is a measured portfolio, never ambient magic. Direct algorithms are
the mandatory oracle. Regex algebra, graph worklists, e-graph equality islands,
CSP, and COP/constraint-optimization candidates all consume the same immutable
problem and return a checked plan plus cost vector or a typed failure. The vector
keeps semantics first, then compilation time/memory, steady and cold latency,
throughput, allocations, resident/code/artifact bytes, cache/branch behavior,
crossing cost, and target capability. Weights are named policies, not hidden
constants. Holdout grammars and unseen regex shapes must preserve decisions or
expose honest recalibration; training-corpus wins cannot establish generality.

Apple ARM assembly follows the narrow-kernel discipline evidenced by dav1d: one
scalar reference, per-capability function tables, genuine handwritten AArch64
kernels separated by operation family, runtime capability dispatch, and a
checkasm-style differential/benchmark harness that forces each lane. Initial
capabilities are scalar and NEON; later dot-product/I8MM/SVE-class paths require
actual supported Apple hardware and OS-safe detection. Every alignment, tail,
short, malformed, multibyte, page-boundary, and aliasing case compares bit-for-bit
or event-for-event with scalar. A kernel lands only with end-to-end improvement;
an architecture-named scalar wrapper, intrinsic-shaped source with scalar codegen,
or microbenchmark-only win is deleted. No x86 route is retained.

“SOTA” is a closing verdict, never a design adjective. It requires named current
controls, identical semantics/corpora, reproducible Apple/aarch64 measurements,
confidence intervals and raw samples, correctness/resource/cost dominance for the
claimed domain, two hostile gestalt passes, and no hidden casualty. A dominated
engine or backend receives BANK/RETIRE even if sophisticated.

Generality closes only after design-frozen heterogeneous holdouts at minimum cover
regex, JSON, CSS L4, BBNF, EBNF, BNF, CSV, math, and Google Sheets; nested
invocation, ambiguity, recovery, Unicode/coordinate crossings, streaming,
incremental edits, generated code, and Rust/TS/WASM/ARM execution are applied where
semantically relevant. A feature that works only for its founding grammar remains
RED.

## Parse-that locks

Artifact truth is a permanent gate over every lock. Source/package structure is a
cross-lock proof plane. Neither can seal independently from the semantics it proves.

### PT-E — execution kernel

Owns immutable parser definitions; invocation source/state; the result algebra;
complete checkpointing; diagnostics and recovery journals; choice-frame commitment;
memoization; left-recursion growth; progress; unwind; and budgets. These are one
transactional algebra and cannot converge as separate locks.

Born-RED proof set:

- parser construction and the evaluator are private; users cannot retain or mutate
  a `Run`, raw step, last-run state, or process-global semantic identifier;
- `parse` names complete-input semantics, `parsePrefix` names prefix semantics, and
  `parseOrThrow` projects the same discriminated result;
- checkpoints restore cursor/value/evidence, diagnostics/recovery, choice frames,
  memo journal, and LR head/stack/grow frontiers as one transaction;
- cut seals the nearest choice-frame identity and cannot leak into an enclosing
  choice through a successful inner branch;
- memo cells are `Empty | Evaluating | Growing | Done`, store entry-relative
  evidence/effect deltas, and replay effects lawfully or reject effectful use;
- direct, indirect, mutual, multiple-occurrence, nullable, recovery, thrown,
  nested-invocation, and budgeted LR fixtures terminate with named outcomes;
- zero-width repetition fails rather than looping;
- no module-global or thread-local execution/diagnostic state affects a call.

### PT-J — JavaScript/TypeScript surface and package

Owns combinator runtime/type agreement, declarations, entrypoints, module identity,
artifact closure, engine cost, and browser/Node package behavior.

Born-RED proof set:

- runtime values inhabit their generic result types for every exported combinator;
- variadic sequence preserves readonly tuple positions and projections are explicit;
- dispatch accepts only its named key domain and rejects overlap/duplicates;
- one ESM instance serves every supported subpath unless a named CJS consumer earns
  dual publication before the breaking cut;
- source, runtime, declaration, and built-chunk graphs have no SCC crossing feature
  ownership; core does not load memo/debug/domain code accidentally;
- clean build -> pack -> isolated install -> type/Node/Vite/browser/tree-shaking
  craters pass; missing or stale artifacts fail rather than skip;
- allocation, inline-cache, cold-start, throughput, and bytes are measured on the
  supported engine before representation freezes.

### PT-C — coordinate, crossing, and safety contract

Owns the versioned semantic manifest, UTF-16/UTF-8/scalar/line/column domains,
TypeScript/Rust/WASM parity, context capabilities, and unsafe-boundary proof.

Born-RED proof set:

- every operation, outcome, diagnostic, progress rule, coordinate unit, and one-home
  exception appears in one machine-readable manifest;
- public JS positions are qualified UTF-16 offsets and checked conversion maps own
  crossings to Rust byte spans;
- shared fixtures compare values, spans, furthest failure, expected sets, recovery,
  commitment, and budget outcomes over multibyte sources;
- no safe Rust API can construct state later consumed by unchecked invalid slicing,
  SIMD loading, context casting, or aliasing;
- deliberate low-level/engine-specific one-home exceptions have parity fixtures;
- WASM is selected only by resident-boundary measurements, not assumed symmetry.

### PT-K — Rust kernel and scanners

Owns checked spans, typed run/arena ownership, safe recursion, parser primitives,
scanner taxonomy, scalar and Apple/aarch64/NEON paths, memory ownership, Cargo
topology, and the no-x86 law.

Born-RED proof set:

- a clean standalone locked workspace resolves without bbnf-owned patches or
  lockfile mutation;
- arbitrary byte spans, debug-only padded witnesses, default dangling slices,
  erased wrong-context casts, recursive `UnsafeCell` aliasing, and TLS diagnostics
  have no safe path;
- default parsing does not copy the full input; a padded carrier may return only
  after a private/unsafe verified constructor and paired measured win;
- one scanner module owns quote parity, structural bitmap, numbers, digits,
  identifiers, comments, Unicode, and quoted decoding;
- every x86 target, intrinsic, feature, CI lane, emitter, generated path, test, and
  stale claim is removed;
- scalar and genuine NEON paths are differential-equivalent on aligned, unaligned,
  short, malformed, tail, and multibyte inputs;
- useful SpanParser scanner kinds transpose into the one kernel; its duplicate
  parser/combinator surface and Rust CSS grammar delete after fixtures exist;
- inert features and architecture-named scalar fallbacks do not survive.

### PT-R — regex and generation

Owns regex grammar and syntax, canonical HIR, Unicode, NFA/prioritized VM/DFA tiers,
anchors, laziness, captures, match policy, minimization, generated lowering, and
bootstrap reproducibility.

Born-RED proof set:

- one BBNF grammar generates one authoritative syntax parser; the handwritten route
  exists only during differential transposition and then deletes;
- raw scalars, classes, ranges, escapes, anchors, laziness, captures, empty
  alternatives, and invalid inputs have a pinned differential oracle;
- invalid escapes are hard errors and class lowering is exhaustive;
- DFA admits only semantics it preserves; assertions, ordering, and captures use a
  preserving VM tier or fail explicitly;
- full pinned Unicode tables lower to valid byte sequences and spans;
- direct table construction/minimization compares with egraph/CSP and the latter
  survive only with a measured bounded advantage;
- bootstrap is acyclic: `bbnf seed -> generated regex parser -> parse-that -> full
  bbnf`, reaches byte identity twice from an empty output tree, and needs no Python
  rewrite, cargo-expand ambiguity, untracked patch, or reverse provider edge.

### PT-V — Value adoption

Owns a real packed consumer edge, frozen CSS corpus, API migration, string/token/fused
input experiment, semantic suitability, invocation counts, and measured uplift.

Born-RED proof set:

- current Value 4 remains a disconnected control rather than being described as a
  parse-that consumer;
- released control, restored historical control, and candidate use identical pinned
  inputs and packed artifacts;
- value, diagnostic, span, recovery, and coordinate parity precede performance;
- direct string, materialized token cursor, and generated fused scanning compare
  end-to-end; no ABI is selected from microbenchmarks;
- Value depends on the tarball with no source import or adapter; Keyframes owns no
  grammar edge;
- throughput, cold start, allocation, bytes, invocation count, maintainability, and
  casualties are all reported; regressions cannot hide under a broad threshold.

artifact truth -> C0 semantic contract
artifact truth -> PT-E -> PT-J
artifact truth -> PT-K
C0 -> PT-E / PT-J / PT-K / PT-R
PT-E + PT-K -> PT-R
PT-E + PT-J + PT-K + PT-R -> C1 parity closure

## Bbnf-lang locks

### BB-1 — type and constraint spine

Owns type syntax, variables, substitutions, unification, schemes, rows, recursive
types, grammar-specific obligations, provenance, diagnostics, and solved layouts.

Born-RED proof set:

- the finite shape classifier is renamed or replaced; no claim of HM inference is
  made without variables, generalization, instantiation, and principal-type tests;
- every domain operation satisfies identity, idempotence, commutativity,
  associativity, and monotonicity property tests where its algebra requires them;
- constraint order and hash iteration cannot change a solution or diagnostic;
- occurs-check and explicit recursive types cannot be confused;
- closed records, open rows, variants, tuples, lists, optionality, functions,
  spans, and user transforms have named unification rules;
- every fallback is an error or a named recovery obligation; no `Span` default and
  no ignored solver result remain;
- a solved `TypeSolution` is immutable and consumed through one interface;
- parse-that result types and the Value fabric are real consumers in the same
  tranche that introduces the needed type feature.

### BB-2 — dependency graph, e-graph, and scheduling

Owns typed dependency edges, SCC condensation, fixed-point scheduling, equality
saturation islands, CSP use, invalidation, and cost evidence.

Born-RED proof set:

- one canonical graph stores node identity and typed producer -> artifact ->
  consumer edges; ad hoc Tarjan and union-find variants are removed or justified;
- legal grammar recursion is distinguished from illegal module/package cycles and
  bounded compiler feedback;
- direct worklist, CSP, and e-graph scheduling are compared on identical facts;
- the e-graph is retained only for rewrite families whose equivalence and cost
  extraction need it; it is never the ambient mutable IR;
- errors are propagated and fixed points have explicit monotone measures and
  iteration budgets;
- incremental invalidation equals clean recomputation on randomized edit traces;
- deterministic JSON, DOT, and condensation reports are byte-identical across two
  clean runs.

### BB-3 — IR and staging spine

Owns parsing output, canonical grammar, analysis products, lowering plans,
residual programs, code generation contracts, and artifact provenance.

Born-RED proof set:

- `GrammarIR` is split into immutable `Grammar`/`Program` input and explicit
  `DependencyGraph`, `TypeSolution`, `AnalysisFacts`, and `LoweringPlan` outputs;
- no pass mutates another pass's hidden sidecar or caches pointer-derived identity;
- every stage consumes a value and returns a value or a named error;
- interpreted, VM, and generated routes share a semantic program where measured
  useful; source emission is not used as the interchange representation;
- generated artifacts carry source digest, semantic-manifest digest, generator
  version, and reproducible command;
- code generation cannot read mutable pre-analysis state after a solution is
  sealed;
- any contract-tower layer with no independent invariant is collapsed.

### BB-7 — grammar totality and bootstrap

Owns the BBNF language, self-description, host-function boundary, bootstrap stages,
grammar packages, and total corpus.

Born-RED proof set:

- language syntax and semantics have one versioned specification and negative
  corpus;
- bootstrap generation from the seed reaches a byte-identical fixed point twice;
- host functions are declared with typed signatures, effects, ownership, and
  backend availability; arbitrary host callbacks cannot bypass the type spine;
- BBNF, EBNF, BNF, JSON, CSS, CSV, math, and Sheets grammars are either product
  fixtures with owners and gates or are deleted;
- grammar-driven regex syntax is used to regenerate the regex parser before the
  regex generator may serve BBNF output;
- parse-that comparison and dogfood distinguish handwritten, generated, and hybrid
  arms on semantics, diagnostics, size, cold start, throughput, and maintainability.

## Structural graph plane

Structure is a cross-lock proof plane rather than a tenth feature lock. Its typed
multigraph contains:

- Cargo packages and features;
- Rust modules, re-exports, generated-file provenance, and test ownership;
- TypeScript modules and resolved imports;
- Vue SFC script/style/template component edges;
- grammar sources, generators, generated artifacts, packages, WASM glue, and
  runtime consumers;
- documentation authority and supersession edges.

Clusters are sized from SCCs, articulation points, high fan-in/out, directories,
and feature ownership. Each cluster is reviewed by two hostile critics and a fresh
adjudicator. A move is accepted only when it lowers ambiguity or coupling without
creating a god module or sand. Within a feature module, filenames omit the module
name (`easing/option`, not `easing/easing-option`). Tests live outside production
trees in a source-isomorphic path.

## Gestalt passes

### GESTALT-P1 — parse-that totality

Weaves historical LOCK-4/5/6/8/9 through their current PT-E/J/C/K/R/V homes after
their first evidence-backed transpositions. It proves one run model, one result
algebra, one regex home, one package identity, one scanner policy, and one semantic
manifest. Findings route back to their exact lock-coverage rows.

### GESTALT-P2 — parse-that dirigibility

Runs after P1 corrections. It starts from a packed TypeScript artifact and a clean
Rust checkout, regenerates artifacts, executes shared fixtures, and performs the
three-arm Value dogfood. Two consecutive clean audits are required.

### GESTALT-B1 — bbnf-lang totality

Weaves BB-1/2/3/7 around one immutable pipeline. It proves there is one canonical
product plane, one fact authority, one graph, one type solution, one lowering plan,
and one bootstrap story. Root/skinny/wasm duplication receives a whole-plane
disposition.

### GESTALT-B2 — bbnf-lang dirigibility

Runs after B1 corrections. A new contributor must be able to trace grammar source
to parser result and demo rendering through named values and artifact edges without
hidden mutation. Clean bootstrap, full tests, benchmarks, and browser flows must
pass twice with identical graph and generated outputs.

### GESTALT-omega1 — combined product

Composes every lock through real package boundaries:

`regex grammar -> parse-that generator/runtime -> BBNF grammar/parser -> typed IR
-> analysis/lowering -> emitted parser -> Value -> Keyframes -> exact packed Glass
7.0.0 -> every-page assayed demo`.

It rejects workspace-source shortcuts, duplicate identities, coordinate ambiguity,
any generated artifact whose provenance cannot be reproduced, a surviving Glass
3.x face, or any route/template/state omitted from frontend-design and browser
proof.

### GESTALT-omega2 — novelty and closure

Runs after omega1 corrections. It proves the campaign produced a capability absent
from the starting trees: grammar-driven regex bootstrap feeding a typed staged BBNF
pipeline and a visibly exercised consumer path. It replays all lock gates, graph
audits, package installs, bootstrap hashes, differential corpora, performance
budgets, and browser journeys. Two clean independent audits close the campaign;
anything else supersedes this constitution with a new pass.
