# BB-C.W0 — remove ambient optimizer authority

## Goal

Stop unsound or partial optimization from rewriting production semantics before a
replacement wins a bounded proof.

## Implementation

1. Delete enabled G3 (`Alt([x, ε]) -> x`) with its empty-input/value/span/
   diagnostic counterexample; do not retain it disabled or renamed.
2. Make incomplete saturation, extraction failure, missing child/best node, cycle,
   cap, timeout, and translation mismatch typed failures with complete reports.
3. Disable ambient whole-program e-graph writeback and independent-minimum CSP
   fallback. Production uses direct semantics until a later island passes gates.
4. Inventory every egraph/CSP/optimizer call site, its decision language, hard
   constraints, consumer, and current failure behavior.
5. Add mutations showing a discarded hard constraint, partial result, or silent
   fallback reaches output; each must fail before later work.

## Gates

- No production semantic rewrite runs without a declared bounded language,
  validator, and report.
- G3 and all fallback symbols/routes are absent by source/graph census.
- Incumbent behavior remains through direct unoptimized semantics, or a typed
  “optimization unavailable” result—not a partial rewrite.
- All mutation witnesses invert and artifacts remain deterministic.

## Completion

Correct direct semantics is the baseline; complex engines have no inherited right
to production.
