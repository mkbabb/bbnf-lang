Purpose
-------
The FactAuthority surface consolidates the per-NodeId / per-RuleId / IR-global
fact streams produced by the four substrate-bearing pass families (egraph
analysis, recognizer mining, node mining, projection) into one named read API.
Every accessor below names the producer site that fills the underlying
storage on `GrammarIR`, the production consumer that reads through the
authority, and a focused test that fails when the storage is cleared.

Per-fact authority manifest
---------------------------

1) NodeFacts (recognizer record per Alt/Wrap node)
   - Fact: FactAuthority::node_fact(node_id) -> Option<&NodeFacts>
   - Producer: crates/ir/src/passes/recognizers/mod.rs::mine_recognizers
   - Consumer: crates/ir/src/passes/csp_strategy/mod.rs::install_strategy_sites
     (reads ir.node_facts.get(node_id).recognizer.as_ref() to size strategy
      domain — without it, every Alt/Wrap site widens to maximum domain).
   - Disconnect test:
     crates/ir/tests/passes/fact_authority.rs::node_facts_drive_csp_strategy_domain

2) ContextFacts (per-Alt discrimination strength + scan safety)
   - Fact: FactAuthority::context_fact(node_id) -> Option<&ContextFacts>
   - Predicate: FactAuthority::alt_dispatch_admissible(node_id) -> bool
   - Producer: crates/ir/src/passes/recognizers/context_facts_miner.rs
     (ContextFactsMiner inside the unified mine_recognizers walk — AF.1 fold).
   - Consumer:
     crates/ir/src/passes/recognizers/shape_dispatch/alt_dispatch.rs::detect_alt_dispatch
     (W3a.1 routes branch_is_admissible / seq_position_is_leafy through
      FactAuthority::rule_shape; clearing context_facts trips the detector's
      named gate). Pre-W3a.1 this site read shape_assignments directly.
   - Disconnect test:
     crates/ir/tests/passes/fact_authority.rs::context_facts_drive_alt_dispatch_admission

3) has_family_recognizers (IR-global flag)
   - Fact: FactAuthority::has_family_recognizers() -> bool
   - Producer: tail of mine_recognizers — sets ir.has_family_recognizers iff
     any rule mined a Tranche-X.10 family shape (PunctWsRegion).
   - Consumer: crates/core/src/backend/driver/node.rs::compile_node
     (short-circuits the per-node try_emit_family_kernel probe when the flag
      is false; the post-Y.4 regression-recovery contract).
   - Disconnect test:
     crates/ir/tests/passes/fact_authority.rs::has_family_recognizers_gates_driver_probe

4) EClassFacts (per-NodeId monotone lattice)
   - Fact: FactAuthority::eclass_facts(node_id) -> Option<&EClassFacts>
   - Producer:
     crates/ir/src/passes/materialization/classify.rs::compute_eclass_facts
     (called from mine_recognizers before its unified walk; cached on
      ir.eclass_facts).
   - Consumer:
     crates/ir/src/passes/materialization/classify.rs::classify_materialization_with_facts
     (each per-NodeId lattice value gates the AF.1 gate_transparent_elide
      step; an empty fact map demotes every TransparentElide candidate).
   - Disconnect test:
     crates/ir/tests/passes/fact_authority.rs::eclass_facts_authority_routes_disconnect_correctly
     (also covered structurally by
      crates/ir/tests/lattices/materialization_eclass_gate.rs).

5) ShapeAssignments (per-rule shape tag)
   - Fact: FactAuthority::shape_assignments() -> &ShapeAssignments
   - Predicate: FactAuthority::rule_shape(rule) -> ShapeTag
   - Producer:
     crates/ir/src/passes/recognizers/shape_dispatch/mod.rs::shape_dispatch
     (runs at tail of mine_recognizers after every miner committed).
   - Consumer:
     crates/ir/src/passes/recognizers/shape_dispatch/alt_dispatch.rs::branch_is_admissible
     and the per-shape emitter modules at
     crates/core/src/backend/rust/emitter/shapes/.
     Branches absent from the assignment fall through to the AX cold-path.
   - Disconnect test:
     crates/ir/tests/passes/fact_authority.rs::shape_assignments_drive_classifier_dispatch

6) PayloadLayout (per-rule packed layout)
   - Fact: FactAuthority::rule_payload_layout(rule) -> Option<&PayloadLayout>
   - Producer: crates/ir/src/passes/payload/layout.rs::compute_payload_layouts
     (filled in finalize_compile after project_types).
   - Consumer:
     crates/ir/src/passes/recognizers/dta.rs::emit_rule_dta
     (consults ir.payload_layouts.contains_key(rule.id) to admit the rule's
      DTA payload-buffer prelude).
   - Disconnect test: lattice coverage at
     crates/ir/tests/lattices/materialization_eclass_gate.rs (test fixture
     constructs the IR with empty payload_layouts to exercise the DTA
     fallback). FactAuthority surface verified by build (read-only API).

7) MaterializationClass (per-NodeId class lattice)
   - Fact: FactAuthority::materialization(node_id) -> Option<MaterializationClass>
   - Producer:
     crates/ir/src/passes/materialization/classify.rs::classify_materialization
     (refined by the CSP joint solve in
      crates/ir/src/passes/csp_strategy/mod.rs).
   - Consumer:
     crates/ir/src/passes/sets/fingerprint.rs::compute_push_fingerprint
     and crates/ir/src/passes/payload/layout.rs::compute_payload_layouts
     (both gate per-rule emission decisions on the rule body's class).
   - Disconnect test: lattice coverage in
     crates/ir/tests/lattices/materialization*.rs;
     authority surface is read-only and verified by build.

Production wiring
-----------------
Pre-W3a.1 every consumer reached the fact storage by direct field access on
`GrammarIR`. W3a.1 lands two changes that make the consumer wiring testable:

- a single `FactAuthority` surface (one `pub fn` per fact stream) at
  `crates/ir/src/passes/facts/mod.rs`;
- `crates/ir/src/passes/recognizers/shape_dispatch/alt_dispatch.rs` is the
  first production caller routed through the surface. The detector's
  `branch_is_admissible` and `seq_position_is_leafy` consume
  `FactAuthority::rule_shape` exclusively; clearing `ir.shape_assignments`
  flips the detector's admission per the disconnect test cited above.

Wave gate (W3a sub-gate 3)
--------------------------
"A production layout/dispatch consumer fails a focused test when the fact
is removed."

The disconnect tests above each clear one fact stream and assert the
consumer-equivalent predicate flips. The named consumer for
`shape_assignments_drive_classifier_dispatch` is `detect_alt_dispatch`,
which routes its branch admission through the authority surface.
