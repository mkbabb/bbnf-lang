The Era V failure mode (`docs/tranches/meta-audit/archaeology/era-V-dta-psi-rut.md:7-10`) — substrate-first-consumer-later — does NOT apply here because every relocation has a same-wave consumer: the layered structure is consumed by `cargo check --workspace` immediately, the pipeline directory-form by every `mod pipeline` consumer, the `host/css_types.rs` move by the existing CSS L4 grammar's `crate::css_types::parse_hex_color` reference (which rewrites to `crate::host::css_types::parse_hex_color`). No substrate without consumer lands in W0; the layered re-org IS the consumer of itself.

The 9-directory partition is purposive. `source/` houses input-layer concerns: AST, directives, imports, dep-graph (per `audit/MODULES-2026-05-03.md:1109` "types + directives + imports + dep-graph"). `parse/` houses BBNF self-host parse + CstSchema + generated/ (per same:1110). `lower/` houses BBNF AST → IR (per same:1111). `codegen/` is the merger of today's `generate/` + `backend/` (per same:1112) consumed by the BA.W2 god-module splits. `runtime/` houses per-grammar arenas / builders / documents / views (per same:1113). `path/` houses the typed-path executor (per same:1114). `pipeline/` houses the compile orchestrator (per same:1115). `host/` houses host-fn shims like `css_types` (per same:1116). `lib.rs` is the re-export hub (per same:1117). The earlier user-draft proposal that included `scan/`, `ast/`, `ir/`, `ir_pass/`, `typing/`, `optimize/` directories per `audit/MODULES-2026-05-03.md:1078-1104` is rejected: `scan/` belongs to `simd-scan`, the AST is the bootstrap parse result, and IR/optimise live in `bbnf-ir`. The corrected 9-directory shape is smaller and describes the actual contents of core.
