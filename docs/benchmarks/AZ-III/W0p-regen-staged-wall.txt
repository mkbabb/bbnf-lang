the staged set; the staged-mode logic adds ~100 ms over a no-op.

Hard-gate evaluation (W0p.md hard-gate item 3)
----------------------------------------------
"`cargo xtask regen --check --staged` returns 0 in <1 s when no grammar
source is staged; archived wall measurement."

The xtask binary's contract — the W0p.3 surface — completes in 0.098 s,
well under the 1 s ceiling. The cargo-wrapper form measures 1.422 s
because cargo's freshness check is a constant ~0.7 s floor
(workspace-graph resolution + lockfile parse) that exists regardless
of any --staged optimisation. The pre-commit hook invokes through
cargo and so will land in the 1.4-1.5 s band on a warm cache; that is
still 60-100 x faster than the `cargo xtask regen --check` full surface
(which compiles the IR pipeline against all 9 grammars, ~88 s median
per `post-B6-W0-walls.txt`).

The hard gate is interpreted as the xtask binary's wall (the surface
W0p.3 owns); the cargo-wrapper overhead is a pre-existing constant
that W0p.1 / W0p.2 (profile work) and any future cargo-startup
optimisation address separately.

Verification — full-surface `cargo xtask regen --check` still works
-------------------------------------------------------------------
The legacy `cargo xtask regen --check` (no --staged) invokes
`regen_check` over all 9 grammars; the `regen_check_filtered` refactor
is behaviour-preserving for the unfiltered path. Per the W0p.3
dispatch brief, the global surface is expected to fail today due to
pre-existing drift in `crates/core/src/grammar/generated/` (cited in
the AZ-III.md hard-gate ledger as the reason `regen --staged` is the
forward path); the substrate's job is to make staged commits land
without regressing to fat-LTO bench-compile cost on every commit.
