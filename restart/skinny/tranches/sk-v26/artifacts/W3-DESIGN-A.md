## Boundary defects

The pass identified seven concrete gaps:

1. W0 did not emit the promised public-witness feature matrix or stable IDs.
2. No grammar-instance carrier or schema exists.
3. `Program::resolve` chooses a rule named `root`, otherwise the first rule.
4. The selected frontend lacks comments, braces, brackets, projections,
   difference, `?w`, and presentation hints required by the known feature
   union.
5. No genuine Pratt representation, plan, event, descriptor, or emitter path
   exists.
6. parse-that exposes no operation capable of transactional set difference.
7. No exact freeze closure, frozen receipt, or zero-diff verifier exists.

## Proposed spine

Design A proposed:

- a corrective W0 public-witness addendum with stable JSON, CSS, and Sheets
  IDs and exact positive, negative, recovery-policy, and full-consumption
  cases;
- one parse-that `difference` combinator, version `0.5.0`, and
  `KERNEL_ABI == 3`;
- clean-break `compile_instance`, explicit entry identity, strict instance
  validation, and revision hashing over source plus semantic instance data;
- grammar-source comments, braces, brackets, projections, difference, `?w`,
  and finite inert presentation hints;
- new semantic/lowering ownership for projection, difference, trivia, and
  Pratt;
- compact revision-bound Pratt table/operator IDs, binding-power execution,
  progress and resource enforcement, transactional operator attempts, and
  generated descriptors;
- one generic external public-witness consumer with no per-format feature,
  dependency, runtime directory, callback, or handwritten parser;
- deliberate replay/schema version advances;
- two hostile pre-freeze reviews plus fresh adjudication;
- a git-durable freeze basis and receipt before executing the sealed held-out
  selection;
- git-object zero-diff verification, dual-empty-directory regeneration, and
  mutation self-tests after selection.

## Falsifiers

The proposed pre-freeze falsifiers included:

- explicit entry wins even when a rule named `root` exists;
- consistent rule/path renaming preserves normalized behavior;
- every forbidden instance field rejects;
- changing semantic instance data changes `Revision`;
- a neutral grammar exercises the complete syntax and Pratt surface;
- excluded difference events never survive;
- overlapping operators have deterministic priority;
- generated output contains no raw grammar, format identity, plugin, callback,
  or logical-path switch;
- one harness generates all public witnesses;
- the post-freeze proof reports exact frozen-path equality rather than a grep
  allowlist.

## Items left to adjudication

Design A intentionally left the following proposals open to the final pass:

- whether normalization or recovery belong in the instance schema;
- whether trivia is trailing-only or two-sided;
- whether projections need two variants or one operation with a keep side;
- whether successful Pratt matching needs both operator and reduction events;
- whether explicit empty plugin/dispatch registries add evidence or ceremony;
- whether candidate manifests and cases must be sealed before freeze;
- the exact Pratt attachment site, priority, budget, and failure algorithm.

This document is design evidence only. It authorizes no product mutation and
does not advance or evaluate held-out selection.
