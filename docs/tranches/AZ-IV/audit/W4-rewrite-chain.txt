T3 Transposition (folded in)
----------------------------

Per AUDIT-F transposition T3, the inline-trace recording channel was
lifted from additive `_with_trace` wrappers into the canonical pass
form via `&mut dyn TraceSink`. The W2.2 inline trace and W2.4 path!
macro consumers continue to function unchanged; the production caller
in pipeline/compile.rs threads an InlineTrace through the canonical
inline_acyclic / fuse_single_use signature directly.

Implementation:
- Added `TraceSink` trait + `NoopTraceSink` zero-overhead unit type
  to crates/ir/src/passes/inline_trace.rs.
- `InlineTrace` impls `TraceSink`.
- `inline_acyclic` and `fuse_single_use` signatures changed from
  `(ir: &mut GrammarIR)` (with sibling _with_trace wrapper) to
  `(ir: &mut GrammarIR, trace: &mut dyn TraceSink)`.
- _with_trace wrappers + _inner private helpers deleted.
- Public exports in passes/mod.rs and passes/transform/mod.rs updated.

Closes mid-tranche §R2: inline-trace pipeline-ordering coupling
(AUDIT-F finding) — the canonical pass form *is* the recording form;
there is no longer a discipline question of which entry point to call.
