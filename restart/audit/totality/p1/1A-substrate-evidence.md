## Executive Summary

The skinny substrate implements the center of Lock 1 for JSON: `Tape<'input>` owns source-backed offset storage, generated `JsonRoot` owns a sealed tape, and `ValueRef<'doc, 'input, K>` is the tape-plus-cursor borrow shape. `BackendShape` also has the five specified variants. The divergence is that the live runtime is still an offset-tape JSON/direct prototype, not the full V1 substrate family. The claimed 20-variant BIR alphabet is not present: live `BackendExpr` has 13 variants and moves `SimdScan` into a separate recognizer list. `EventTape`, `CollapsedStage`, `LayoutScope`, `CallHost`, `ErrorRecover`, `PathEval`, `DebugMark`, and a runtime typed event cursor are absent in the audited scope. More seriously for Lock 1, generated JSON retained parse and `parse_direct` are separate functions, while the CSS declaration-values runtime emits a fact stream with no tape at all. These are current-state divergences, not proposed fixes. Estimated closure is medium-to-high risk: roughly 1.5K-3K LOC for BIR/runtime completeness, plus larger generated-template and benchmark risk outside this agent's scope.

Estimated closure: 400-900 LOC, medium/high risk.
