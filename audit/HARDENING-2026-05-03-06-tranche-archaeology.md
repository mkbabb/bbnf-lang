## Chronic Risk Summary

Four gate clusters have repeated category (a) or (b) history and are the risk surfaces BA must arm against:

1. **Direct projection repeatedly became builder/arena indirection**: `e0e0af30` / `43ea56bb` / `85cf83e7` land `StructRegistry`, `project_types` population, `StructBuilder`, and JSON value substrate; `43f0795b` later records 8/9 StructDirect activation; AZ-IV.W5.3 `0744c9f9` dedups the arena/builder template. Each leaves runtime `StructLayout`, `OpenFrame`, and builder dispatch alive.
2. **Lazy path repeatedly landed as substrate, not the API**: AZ-IV.W3 (`6c9967ae`, `1bd05e8f`, `0e8dbc10`, `5cd6e5d9`) lands typed path IR, path plans, parse_with entry points, and a LazyLock eager path; `bbnf_get_twitter` still calls eager parse and remains thousands of times behind sonic-rs.
3. **Evidence gates repeatedly closed with named misses**: AZ-III and AZ-IV close commits (`c3d4e029`, `203c42d1`, `6de6ac0c`) record terminal / complete-with-misses states; BA must not let perf gates close on existence, partial matrices, or environmental gating.
4. **Deletion gates were often redirected into dedup or whitelisting**: AZ-IV.W5.4 `407cdc8e` makes a permanent substrate audit, and W6.2 `eca339d5` routes 32 zero-caller substrates. BA.W0/W2/W5 must delete or name live consumers, not broaden the whitelist.

## Gate Families With Multiple Prior Misses

### A. Direct-Projection Gates 5, 8, 9, 13

Prior cluster: `e0e0af30`, `43ea56bb`, `85cf83e7`, `1c6f00d0`, `41dd776e`, `43f0795b`, `0744c9f9`.

These are the chronic core. `StructRegistry` and `project_types` did land, and StructDirect did activate across most grammars. The failure was not lack of substrate; it was that the substrate was consumed through a runtime builder and arena, then deduped into templates. BA differs only if W1/W2 make `StructRegistry` a codegen input whose output is typed document/value emit, with runtime layout literals and builder templates gone.

### B. Lazy/SOTA-Path Gates 4, 7, 10, 11, 12, 14, 15, 16, 17

Prior cluster: `916182dd`, `8ad209f0`, `6c9967ae`, `1bd05e8f`, `0e8dbc10`, `5cd6e5d9`, `d8413f68`.

AZ-IV built much of the path substrate, but it did not collapse the API. The failure mode is substrate-without-hot-path-consumer: eager parse remained the benchmarked value API, `LegacyPath` bridged typed paths back to runtime paths, `__path_plan` re-export wrappers survived, and `__EAGER_EMPTY_PATH<Json,_>` leaked a cross-grammar marker. BA differs only if `JsonParser::get<T>` and peers are the canonical entry and `Document::get<T>` no longer walks a materialized AST for narrow paths.

### C. Evidence/Close Gates 1, 2, 3, 6, 18, 19, 20, 23, 24

Prior cluster: `57537137`, `56884188`, `407cdc8e`, `aa31fe56`, `d4b8de18`, `203c42d1`, `6de6ac0c`, `eca339d5`.

The project has progressively better evidence machinery, but closes still admitted misses, routed denominators, and environmental deferrals. BA must be stricter than the historical close pattern: perf claims need samply artefacts, nextest must be 100%, substrate audit must be green, and FINAL must cite resolving artefacts before status flips.

### D. Cleanup/Decision Gates 21, 22

Prior cluster: `c727df9e`, `b82f1439`, B0/B1 dev-loop commits, AZ-IV W6.2 route.

These are not inherently hard engineering tasks; they are process-risk tasks. Their prior failure is survival as "useful later" or "known miss" residue. BA.W0 correctly puts both at the front of the tranche; the risk is accepting a rationale without a concrete consumer or fixture test.

## Conclusion

BA.W0 is not ready to execute unless the hardening synthesis preserves the gate strictness in `docs/tranches/BA/BA.md:135-158`. The archaeology supports BA's thesis: every major prior attempt either deleted the wrong substrate (tape/visitor/Parsed), deduped the surviving runtime indirection, or landed lazy/path substrate without making it the value API. BA is differentiated by three mechanisms that prior tranches lacked in combination: inverse layout audit before emission, direct typed document/value emission consuming `StructRegistry`, and `parse_with` as the single value API path with samply-backed close evidence.
