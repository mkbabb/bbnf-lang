W2 retires the `bbnf_derive` proc-macro crate. The `crates/derive/`
tree deletes outright; the `bbnf_derive` dependency entries purge
from every workspace `Cargo.toml`; the `[patch.crates-io]

## Generated-file regeneration (worktree-local)

Pre-W2 the `crates/core/src/grammar/generated/<ident>.rs` files
shipped from W1 close embedded `include_str!("/Users/mkbabb/
Programming/bbnf-wt-b2-w1/grammar/...")` absolute paths from the
W1 worktree's regen invocation. The W1 close audit flagged this
class explicitly ("rerunning produces only the BBNF self-host's
absolute-path correction"); it surfaces as `os error 2` on every
non-W1-worktree build.

Phase 3's workspace check failed on these stale paths before the
xtask could re-run (the xtask path-deps on `bbnf` itself; bbnf
doesn't compile until paths resolve). One-shot `sed` swap of the
nine generated files restored the build; `cargo xtask regen`
re-emitted the full sweep idempotently in the worktree's path
context (1:25 wall, 9 grammars).

The cherry-pick to master leaves these path swaps OUT of the
cherry-pick set: the regenerated paths would embed
`/Users/mkbabb/Programming/bbnf-wt-b2-w2`, which is just as stale
on master as `bbnf-wt-b2-w1` was. The path-embedding question is
an xtask emission concern (workspace-relative or
`env!("CARGO_MANIFEST_DIR")`), forward-routed to a successor wave
under its own scope. Master inherits the W1 close stale-path state
and resolves it on first regen at any consumer's worktree —
unchanged from pre-W2.

## Archaeology

The proc-macro's role across the codebase's history:

- **Pre-W (early tranches)**: `bbnf_derive` introduced as the
  generative substrate. `BBNF_SCHEMA_VERSION` added at
  `crates/derive/src/lib.rs:81` to invalidate cache on schema
  shifts.
- **AY-I**: 30 423-line `generated.rs` for the BBNF self-host,
  emitted via the proc-macro contract.
- **AY-II.W0/W0'**: IR pipeline accumulation pushed `generated.rs`
  to 33 293 lines + drove the per-derive cold-expand wall from
  ~10-15 min to 80+ min cold.
- **B1**: triumvirate fixed gorgeous's 6-derive serial expansion;
  flagged the bootstrap-side wall as out-of-scope and routed to
  AZ-I.W0 (derive-cache + Watt — the 2026-04-25 deep audit
  subsequently established neither would actually fix the
  cold-miss expansion).
- **2026-04-25 deep audit**: AUDIT-D recommended T3 (xtask +
  checked-in generation), retiring the proc-macro entirely.
- **B2.W0**: xtask substrate landed; first per-grammar emission
  for the BBNF self-host; legacy 33 K-line monolithic
  `generated.rs` deleted in favour of the per-grammar directory.
- **B2.W1**: 62 consumer derive sites across 43 files migrated
  to the `pub use` contract; 8 remaining grammars regenerated
  clean.
- **B2.W2 (this close)**: `crates/derive/` deletes; `bbnf_derive`
  purges from every Cargo.toml; `BBNF_SCHEMA_VERSION` retires;
  the workspace builds clean without the proc-macro.

The deletion at W2 closes the chapter. Future grammar additions
plug into `[workspace.metadata.bbnf]` and run `cargo xtask
regen` — no proc-macro contract.
