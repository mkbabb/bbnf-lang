# Restart Lacuna — Architecture

An audit of the restart frame itself: the locks, the crate topology, what the pre-restart era held
correct, and the TW0–TW6 wave manifest. The frame is treated as auditable, not fixed. Every claim
cites `file:line`. The finding in one sentence: **the restart authored ~600K lines of governing
documentation describing a 24-crate greenfield topology, then quietly executed none of it — the code
is frozen at a 13-crate shape dominated by a 247K-LOC god-crate, several locks describe end-states
never reached, and the one plan that is live (the TW manifest) silently abandons the documented
topology without saying so.**

---

## 0. The meta-lacuna: three divergent plans and a 3.7:1 doc-to-code ratio

The restart carries three overlapping, mutually inconsistent executable plans, none of which has
landed a line of code:

1. **The greenfield 24-crate migration** — `restart/ARCHITECTURE.md:68` ("about 24 crates"),
   `restart/ARCHITECTURE.md:655-677` (the explicit member list: `error`, `pipeline`, `source`,
   `grammar`, `passes`, `vm`, `codegen`, `runtime`, `host`, `cost-model`, `path`, `path-core`,
   `parse-that`, `bbnf-simd`, `test-fixtures`, …), with a phased A–J LOC trajectory at
   `restart/MIGRATION.md:891-914`.
2. **The legacy BA–BD inheritance map** — `restart/inheritance/INDEX.md:29-40`, a second A–J tranche
   lettering over the same 24-crate shape.
3. **The SK-V20 TW0–TW6 manifest** — `restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v20/SPEC.md:50-161`, which operates
   on the *existing* 13-crate tree (`crates/core`, `crates/ir`, `crates/simd-scan`) and folds skinny
   into it.

These do not agree on the target topology (see §2). The TW manifest never references a single one of
the 24 greenfield crate names: the only topology instruction it carries is "Port skinny
`crates/runtime/src/tape/` … into `crates/core`" (`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v20/SPEC.md:71`) and
"begin the `core` split (frontend / codegen / regex / runtime)"
(`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v20/SPEC.md:76`). The 24-crate ARCHITECTURE/MIGRATION design —
~280 KB of authoritative text (`restart/ARCHITECTURE.md` 180 KB, `restart/MIGRATION.md` 100 KB) — is
therefore dead-on-arrival: superseded in practice, never marked superseded. This is the single most
consequential lacuna. A reader of ARCHITECTURE.md believes the target is 24 crates; the live plan
targets a 4-way split of `core`. No document reconciles them.

**The volumetric pathology.** `restart/` markdown totals **3,678 files, 598,829 lines, 5.64 million
words**. Hand-written Rust across `crates/` is **159,964 lines** (generated Rust is a further
169,956). That is a **3.74:1 doc-to-code ratio by line, and roughly 35:1 by word** — and the code
side is *frozen*: the TW manifest's own opening state records "cutover commit 0 … was never executed"
(`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v21/PASS-ALPHA.md:3-4`). The legacy corpus adds another 668 files /
176,504 lines (`docs/tranches/`). The campaign's output is documentation about a codebase that has
not moved. No lock caps this ratio; §1 shows why the ones that exist did not bite.

## 1. The locks — stale, unenforced, and vacuously green

`restart/locks/LOCKS.md` (850 lines) codifies sixteen locks at lines 71–580. Audited individually:

### 1a. Lock 14 (zero overfitting) — its falsifier scans crates that do not exist

Lock 14's own verification commands (`restart/locks/LOCKS.md:279`) grep
`crates/{ir,parse,codegen,runtime,path,path-core,egraph,csp-solver,parse-that-regex,parse-that,bbnf-simd,analysis,lsp}/src/`
for grammar-name leaks. Of those thirteen paths, **seven do not exist**: there is no `crates/parse`,
`crates/codegen`, `crates/runtime`, `crates/path`, `crates/path-core`, `crates/bbnf-simd`, or
`crates/parse-that`. They are all fused inside `crates/core` (§2). `ripgrep` over a non-existent path
matches nothing and exits clean, so the falsifier **passes vacuously over the exact surfaces most
likely to hold leaks** — the codegen and runtime code. The DEFERRED-LEDGER concedes this directly:
the W6 Lock-14 gate is "green-by-exclusion" (`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v20/DEFERRED-LEDGER.md:30`).
This is the document-over-runtime pathology the campaign is supposed to have eliminated, embedded in
the anti-overfitting lock itself.

Second Lock-14 defect: it asserts `find crates/core/src/runtime -mindepth 1 -maxdepth 1 -type d`
returns **ZERO** per-grammar dirs (`restart/locks/LOCKS.md:279`). Reality: **ten** exist — `bbnf`,
`bnf`, `css_l4`, `css_pretty`, `csv`, `ebnf`, `google_sheets`, `json`, `math`, `tape`
(`crates/core/src/runtime/`). They are template-emitted (`crates/core/src/runtime/json/value.rs:1`:
`// @generated by xtask regen-json`), so the *spirit* is met — but the literal falsifier is false
against the tree and would fail if ever run. A lock whose falsifier cannot be run as written is not a
lock.

Third Lock-14 defect: the generated-file allowance is keyed to the marker `// @generated`
(`restart/locks/LOCKS.md:290-294`), but the largest generated file uses a *different* marker —
`crates/core/src/grammar/generated/css_l4.rs:1` opens `//! AUTO-GENERATED from …`, not `// @generated`.
A grep-based gate keyed on the `// @generated` string skips the 108,406-line file
(`crates/core/src/grammar/generated/css_l4.rs`) entirely. Marker inconsistency is a gate blind spot on
the single biggest artefact.

### 1b. Lock 12 (ser + gorgeous archive BEFORE A.W0) — precondition never met, campaign ran anyway

Lock 12 states "ser + gorgeous archive BEFORE A.W0. Clean slate is the precondition for the A tranche
to begin" (`restart/locks/LOCKS.md:258`). Neither has been archived: `crates/ser` (530 LOC) is still
a workspace member (`Cargo.toml:2`) and is depended on by `core` (`crates/core/Cargo.toml:17`);
`crates/gorgeous` (1,441 LOC) is still a member and depends on `core` + `ir`
(`crates/gorgeous/Cargo.toml:79-80`). The precondition is unmet, yet ten skinny tranches
(sk-v10…sk-v20) executed regardless. This proves the locks are aspirational prose, not enforced gates:
a hard precondition was violated and nothing stopped the campaign. The DEFERRED-LEDGER re-files this
as an open item (`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v20/DEFERRED-LEDGER.md:47`, N11).

### 1c. Lock 7 (consolidated `crates/path/`) and Lock 11 (parse-that path-dep) — describe crates that were never created

Lock 7 (`restart/locks/LOCKS.md:130`): "`crates/path/` is the consolidated path crate. The runtime
cursor engine merges INTO it." No `crates/path/` exists — only `crates/bbnf-path` (a 918-LOC
proc-macro shell, `crates/core/Cargo.toml:90-95`) and the cursor at `crates/core/src/path`
(2,234 LOC), un-merged. Lock 11 (`restart/locks/LOCKS.md:249`) names `parse-that` /
`parse-that-regex` as incubating path-dep sister crates; neither is a workspace member (`Cargo.toml:2`).
Both locks describe a topology end-state that the executable plan never approaches.

### 1d. Lock 13 (no god directories) — flagrantly violated by the live tree

Lock 13 (`restart/locks/LOCKS.md:266`): "No god directories; cohesive encapsulation at every level.
Every directory partitions one cohesive concern." The live tree violates this at the largest possible
scale: `crates/core` is **247,428 LOC across 440 files**, and its single subtree
`crates/core/src/grammar` is **171,289 LOC** — a god-directory that swallows all nine generated
grammars, the schema, and the emit machinery. `crates/ir` is a second 52,314-LOC monolith. No lock
caps crate or directory size; Lock 13 is qualitative and, as §1b shows, unenforced.

### 1e. The category the locks never contained

The charter's own diagnosis holds and extends: the SK-V20 era exposed that **no lock forced runtime
gates over documents, none capped the doc/code ratio, none bound checkasm to liveness**. To that list
the audit adds: **no lock caps god-crate/god-directory size in enforceable terms** (Lock 13 is
qualitative), **no lock forces topology convergence** (the 24-crate design can rot indefinitely with
no gate firing), and **no lock requires its own falsifier to resolve to a non-empty path set** (Lock
14's vacuity). The missing meta-lock is: *a lock whose verification command matches zero files is
itself a failing lock.*

### 1f. Locks that remain correct

Lock 1's 2026-05-04 tape reframe (`restart/locks/LOCKS.md:71`, the amendment at the paragraph tail),
Lock 4's per-domain orthogonality with the egraph↔csp bridge (`restart/locks/LOCKS.md:113`,
verified: `egraph` has no csp import path — it composes via `passes::bridge`), Lock 6 (xtask emits
committed source, `restart/locks/LOCKS.md:113`), and Lock 16's checkasm-admission discipline
(`restart/locks/LOCKS.md:383`) remain sound in intent and are the ones the TW manifest correctly
carries forward.

### 2a. `core` internal structure — the real decomposition seam

`crates/core/src` (`crates/core/src/lib.rs` + subtrees): `grammar` 171,289 LOC (generated parsers +
schema/emit), `backend` 22,212, `runtime` 8,887, `generate` 4,642, `lower` 4,522, `path` 2,234,
`pipeline` 1,423, `graph` 776, `imports` 566. This single crate contains what ARCHITECTURE.md wanted
as **at least eight separate crates** — `grammar`, `codegen`, `runtime`, `passes`, `pipeline`,
`source`, `path-core`, `host` (`restart/ARCHITECTURE.md:79-91`). The planned decomposition never
began. `crates/ir` similarly fuses what the design splits into `ir` + `passes` + `vm`
(`restart/ARCHITECTURE.md:82-84`).

### 2b. Empty husks and vestiges

- **`bootstrap` (465 LOC)** — self-documents as a retired vestige: "this crate becomes a thin
  re-export … so existing consumers of `bbnf_bootstrap::BbnfBootstrap` continue to resolve"
  (`crates/bootstrap/src/lib.rs:5-12`). Consumed by nobody (`Cargo.toml` scan). Its `bin/` holds three
  debug tools (`dump_ir.rs`, `cost_grid_sweep.rs`, `debug_parse.rs`). Pure deletion candidate; the
  re-export can move to `core`.
- **`analysis` (5,241 LOC)** — the analysis-consolidation (merge into IR passes / into
  `bbnf-language-server`) was designed at `restart/ARCHITECTURE.md:76` ("Consolidates `crates/analysis`
  and `crates/lsp`") and never executed. It is an orphan feeding only `lsp`
  (`crates/lsp/Cargo.toml:23`).
- **`simd-scan` (3,389 LOC)** — still ships `avx2.rs`, `avx512.rs`, `wasm.rs`
  (`crates/simd-scan/src/`) on an aarch64-only project. The W5d deletion (avx2/avx512/wasm out of
  `simd-scan`, `restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v20/SPEC.md:52-53`) never landed; the dead cross-arch code
  is live (`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v20/DEFERRED-LEDGER.md:29`, W5d OPEN).
- **`ser` (530 LOC)** — ARCHITECTURE.md marks it non-surviving ("`ser` and gorgeous … do not survive
  as production crates," `restart/ARCHITECTURE.md:99`); still a live dep of `core`.

### 2c. Is 13 crates the right cut for the TW manifest?

No — but the fix the charter gestures at (a `kernels` crate, a `value` crate, a `tape` crate) is
**already the right instinct and already half-designed** in ARCHITECTURE.md, which names `runtime`
(tape + value + document), `codegen`, `host`, and `bbnf-simd` (kernels) as distinct crates
(`restart/ARCHITECTURE.md:85-96`). The lacuna is not that the design lacks the right cut; it is that
the TW manifest treats a workspace-scale decomposition as an incidental side-effect of the substrate
landing — "Topology moves that the substrate landing forces … begin the `core` split"
(`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v20/SPEC.md:76`) — rather than as a first-class wave with its own gate.
The fused 247K-LOC `core` cannot host a clean tape/value/kernel separation while it also holds the
frontend, the backend walker, the lowering, and the path engine; the value-plane wave (TW3) and the
kernel-plane wave (TW4) will both re-fuse concerns into `core` unless the split precedes them. The
correct sequencing is **decomposition first, then substrate/value/kernel** — the inverse of the
current manifest, where the split is a footnote to TW1.

## 3. What the pre-restart era had RIGHT that the restart dropped

The restart's greenfield framing risks discarding assets the pre-restart tree actually possesses:

1. **The `tape` substrate itself.** The restart's first move was to retire the tape *name* and
   rebrand it `ParseStream` (`restart/inheritance/INDEX.md:66`). Lock 1's 2026-05-04 amendment records
   this as an over-correction: "the prior restart's wholesale retirement of the tape name was an
   over-correction against the implementation failure; the user has confirmed tape is the right
   substrate when implemented properly" (`restart/locks/LOCKS.md:71`, amendment). ARCHITECTURE.md
   concurs — "Keep the term `tape`. `ParseStream` only appears today as a `syn` macro parse type"
   (`restart/ARCHITECTURE.md:49`). The restart dropped and then had to re-adopt what the pre-restart
   era already had correct. The insight the SoA/columnar detour lost time re-litigating: the *concept*
   was never the failure — the 2,000-commit failure was implementation (orthogonal `Vec<OpenFrame>`
   substrates, type ambivalence, consumer-later sequencing), not the tape idea
   (`restart/locks/LOCKS.md:71`).

2. **A working nine-grammar fleet, end-to-end, today.** `crates/core/src/grammar/generated/` carries
   functioning parsers for all nine grammars (`css_l4.rs`, `bbnf.rs`, `google_sheets.rs`,
   `css_pretty.rs`, `ebnf.rs`, `json.rs`, `bnf.rs`, `csv.rs`, `math.rs`) and
   `crates/core/src/runtime/<grammar>/` holds their generated value/view/builder/document accessors.
   This is a real, regen-clean, template-emitted asset. The greenfield's "start informed, not blank"
   posture (`restart/inheritance/INDEX.md:3`) is correct precisely because this fleet exists; the risk
   is the fresh-eyes framing treating it as legacy to be rebuilt rather than a substrate to be
   re-homed.

3. **The disposition realism of MIGRATION.md.** MIGRATION.md §2 is honest about what survives: 121
   files KEEP-OUTRIGHT (generic csp/egraph/simd), 315 ABROGATE-REPLACE (core backend walkers, old
   lowering), and it explicitly warns "The steady-state goal is not 'least LOC'"
   (`restart/MIGRATION.md:323-343`, `:912-914`). This disciplined per-file disposition — the thing the
   pre-restart era produced — is exactly what the TW manifest lacks: TW1–TW6 describe *what to build*
   but not *which of the 834 existing files dies in which commit*. The migration's file-level
   accounting is an asset the wave manifest dropped.

4. **The `@pretty` directive vocabulary and the six-directive V1 grammar** — 30+ extant grammar sites
   use `compact/group/indent/hardbreak/sep/block` (`restart/locks/LOCKS.md:266`, Lock 10 text). This
   is real, deployed surface the restart correctly preserves; noted here as a positive to not regress.

## 4. The TW0–TW6 manifest — orderings, missing waves, optimistic gates

Read fresh (`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v20/SPEC.md:50-170`):

### 4a. Wrong ordering: topology split is buried, not sequenced

Per §2c, the `core` decomposition is a TW1 footnote. The ordering `TW0 → TW1 → TW2 → {TW3 ∥ TW4} →
TW5 → TW6` (`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v20/SPEC.md:165`) lands the value plane (TW3) and kernel plane
(TW4) *inside* the un-split `core`. A dedicated decomposition wave belongs **before TW1**, or TW1 must
be explicitly widened from "begin the split" to "complete the frontend/codegen/runtime/kernel
separation as a gated deliverable." As written, the split is optimistic hand-waving.

### 4b. Missing wave: topology convergence to (or explicit abandonment of) the 24-crate design

There is no wave that reconciles the TW manifest with ARCHITECTURE.md/MIGRATION.md. Either the
24-crate design is the target (then a wave executes it) or it is abandoned (then a doc commit marks
ARCHITECTURE.md §1 and MIGRATION.md §3–§14 superseded). The manifest does neither, leaving ~280 KB of
authoritative-but-dead design in the tree. TW0 is the natural home for the honesty strike ("this SPEC
supersedes the 24-crate topology; the target is the `core` split") — it currently is silent.

### 4c. Optimistic gates

- **TW0's Lock-14 widening** (`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v20/SPEC.md:62-66`) says "scan ALL generic
  surfaces (ir, analysis, passes-equivalents, the codegen crate, xtask)." But "the codegen crate" and
  "passes-equivalents" **do not exist as crates** — they are subtrees of `core`. Until the split
  (§2a), the widened Lock-14 must scan *directory paths inside `core`*, not crate paths. The gate as
  worded inherits Lock 14's vacuity (§1a): it names crates that aren't there. TW0 cannot honestly close
  its Lock-14 leg until the falsifier is re-keyed to `crates/core/src/{backend,generate,lower,…}`.
- **The cutover-gate's "no regression vs pre-fold baseline"** (`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v20/SPEC.md:30-31`)
  presumes a captured pre-fold baseline exists at quiet loadavg. DEFERRED-LEDGER N2 records that ALL
  committed CSS absolute figures ran at loadavg 4.35–6.79 and are "directional only"
  (`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v20/DEFERRED-LEDGER.md:38`). There is no quiet baseline to regress
  against; TW0's "H1 quiet re-capture" must complete *and be trusted* before any TW-gate's
  no-regression clause is meaningful. The gate depends on data that does not yet exist.
- **TW2/TW4 double cutover-gate** (`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v20/SPEC.md:167-170`) is sound in
  concept (static kernels at TW2, cost-model kernels at TW4) but the D11 coupling — that the generator
  un-fork regresses to lightningcss-class speed unless hot leaves stay cost-gated
  (`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v20/SPEC.md:165-168`) — means TW2 can only close "green over a static
  dispatch," i.e. green-over-a-stub, exactly the failure the manifest flags for W6
  (`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v20/SPEC.md:47`). The manifest acknowledges this but does not add a
  gate forbidding a *second* green-over-a-stub at TW2; the risk of a paper-close at TW2 is unguarded.

### 4d. The chronic ledger the manifest inherits is structurally overdue

The DEFERRED-LEDGER's own §1 header concedes "the never-defer precept is violated structurally"
(`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v20/DEFERRED-LEDGER.md:8`). C1 (AoS→SoA tape) has been open since SK-V15,
**4+ cycles** (`:12`); C3 (BackendShape/decision engine) since SK-V15, **5 cycles** (`:14`). These are
not new deferrals — they are the same substrate and decision-engine items that have failed to land
across five tranche cycles. The TW manifest assigns them to TW1/TW4 but offers no evidence that the
force which stalled them five times is now absent. The lacuna is not the assignment; it is the absence
of a diagnosis of *why* C1/C3 never closed — the manifest re-schedules the chronic items without
naming the mechanism that made them chronic (candidate mechanisms surfaced here: the god-crate
prevents clean substrate replacement, §2a; the doc/code ratio absorbs the effort that would land them,
§0; the locks that would force them are vacuous, §1).

### 4e. What the manifest gets right

The manifest's diagnostic content is strong: the three-emit-body un-fork (TW2), the measured
`KernelCostTable` replacing cost-theatre (TW4, `:115-120`), checkasm bound to liveness (TW4,
`:126-129`), the type-system honesty strike before the HM build (TW5, `:137-146`), and every-`->`-
reaches-the-emitter (TW3, `:111-113`) are all correct and evidence-backed by the SK-V20 research
reports (N1–N14, `restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v20/DEFERRED-LEDGER.md:37-50`). The lacuna is not the
*content* of the waves — it is the *frame*: the buried topology split, the un-reconciled 24-crate
design, the gates that name non-existent crates, and the absent diagnosis of chronicity.

## 5. Load-bearing recommendations for the SK-V21 SPEC

1. **Reconcile or bury the 24-crate design explicitly** — a TW0 doc commit that either schedules the
   ARCHITECTURE.md decomposition as a first-class wave or marks it superseded. Silence is the current
   state and it is the worst option.
2. **Promote the `core` split to a gated wave before the value/kernel planes** — decompose the
   247K-LOC `core` along the ARCHITECTURE seams (`grammar`/`codegen`/`runtime`/`host`/`passes`) so TW3
   and TW4 land into clean crates, not into the god-crate.
3. **Re-key every lock falsifier to a path set that resolves to non-empty** — add the meta-lock: a
   verification command matching zero files is a failing lock. Fix Lock 14's crate list to the real
   tree; unify the generated marker to one string.
4. **Delete the husks** — `bootstrap` (re-export → `core`), the x86/wasm bodies in `simd-scan`,
   `ser`+`gorgeous` archival (honour Lock 12 or retire Lock 12's precondition claim).
5. **Cap the doc/code ratio** — a standing gate that a research pass may not add more than N lines of
   markdown per line of code landed. At 3.74:1 and a frozen codebase, the campaign is producing
   documentation, not a parser generator.
6. **Diagnose chronicity before re-scheduling** — for C1/C3 (4–5 cycles open), the SPEC must name the
   mechanism that stalled them, not merely reassign them to TW1/TW4.
