## Tranche fit, feature truth, KISS, and greater-product effect

### Tranche fit

The abstract tranche remains correct. P3.1 is still the smallest coherent next
exporter slice: false foundation authority must be repaired before exact Cargo,
Rust, frontend, generated, WASM, call, or layout work. Expanding the tranche to
semantic promotion or source movement would violate P3 adjudication. The
implementation stays inside a v3.1 clean break and keeps semantic planes Unknown,
which is retained.

This implementation does not complete that tranche. The real constellation
cannot reach graph construction, and foundation inputs, structural evidence, and
publication receipts are still non-authoritative.

### Feature truth

- **Movement:** retain the single denied-only generation latch and false fields in
  every derived view. No separate authorization receipt exists.
- **Schema/hash/Unknown:** retain repository-relative typed identities, full
  edge-record SHA-256 identity, required projection expansion, canonical ordering,
  conflict/dangling rejection, and the distinction between structural validity
  and movement authority.
- **Manifest/inventory:** retain canonical-root alias rejection, the current
  relationship checks, physical-kind checks, and tracked-exclusion rejection;
  repair face/workspace inversion, current documentation authority, actual F08,
  ignored reads, and semantic symlinks.
- **Binding/worktrees:** retain administrative worktree identity, branch spelling,
  tracked and ordinary untracked sensitivity, and measured target resolution;
  repair executable identity, immutable reads, ignored-input enforcement, and
  nomination declaration authority.
- **Projection/cycle/cluster:** retain typed specs, total-node-universe compaction,
  SCC/component identity, and rank-checked component/shard basis kernels; repair
  connected/signal-driven clustering and independently reconstructible quotient
  cycles.
- **Evidence/privacy/resources:** retain new-name staging, direct corruption
  detection, exact directory comparison, normal-path fsync/rename skeleton,
  deterministic synthetic renderers, and recursive privacy walking; repair
  receipt completeness, fallback/same-run durability, universal tokenization,
  and every unavailable full-output gate.
- **Semantic boundary:** retain zero promoted Cargo/Rust/frontend/generated/WASM/
  call exact edges and the honest required-RED fixture ledger. No blanket Unknown
  emission is credited as site execution.

### KISS and structure

Retain the clean v3.1 replacement, the absence of a v3/P2 compatibility route,
the named foundation/cycle/cluster/quotient homes, and external source-isomorphic
tests. Repair the 353-line mixed orchestrator toward the explicit `<=220` target,
split the writer only along proved transaction/rendering invariants, eliminate
the second exclusion authority and duplicate frontend-config binding, and remove
duplicated promoted-bank helpers. This is a Goldilocks repair: it must not create
one-enum sand or a second writer/graph/manifest path.

- the actual bbnf/parse-that constellation cannot be fully exported;
