## Findings

1. **The S-P2 candidate set is research inventory, not a wave selection.**
   P2-A explicitly says its candidates are "not selected waves or an
   implementation order" (`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v12/research/p2/p2a-sota-teardown.md:27`).
   P2-D says no wave selection is made and closes the structural class-lane
   route (`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v12/research/p2/p2d-substrate-tape.md:80`).
   P2-E says the candidates name primitive gaps only and do not select rows or
   implementation order (`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v12/research/p2/p2e-parse-that-gaps.md:46`).
   P2-F says none of its eight families is selected for a wave
   (`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v12/research/p2/p2f-grammar-neutral.md:25`).
   CH3 therefore accepts the research packet only as candidate inventory, not
   as implementation authority.

2. **Retained sidecar / structural-substrate routes remain closed.**
   P2-A treats simdjson's retained structural index as comparator evidence only
   and cites Lock 1 against importing it (`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v12/research/p2/p2a-sota-teardown.md:133`).
   P2-B requires byte-class, structural-terminator, next-bit, and bulk-emission
   masks to be transient and consumed by same-wave callers, not retained
   cursors or side tables (`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v12/research/p2/p2b-dav1d-process.md:37`,
   `:39`, `:45`, `:46`). P2-D explicitly rejects `structural_class_lane_union`
   as REDRESS 96/97/98 terrain (`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v12/research/p2/p2d-substrate-tape.md:78`).
   P2-F restates the sidecar block for F1/F5/F6/F8
   (`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v12/research/p2/p2f-grammar-neutral.md:53`).
   This preserves REDRESS 50/51/53 and REDRESS 96/97/98/102.

3. **Tiny-string, string-span, Unicode, and escaped-segment candidates are
   high-risk but framed with material differentials.**
   The risky candidates are P2-A C2/C3, P2-B `STRING_SPECIAL_BLOCK_CALLER_GATE`
   and `HEX_QUARTET_X4_PROOF_GATE`, P2-C C4/C5, P2-E
   `pt_bounded_plain_string_end` / `pt_hex_quad_decode` /
   `pt_escaped_string_segments`, and P2-F F2/F3
   (`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v12/research/p2/p2a-sota-teardown.md:41`,
   `:53`; `restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v12/research/p2/p2b-dav1d-process.md:40`,
   `:41`; `restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v12/research/p2/p2c-arch-esoterica.md:79`,
   `:87`; `restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v12/research/p2/p2e-parse-that-gaps.md:95`,
   `:222`, `:284`; `restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v12/research/p2/p2f-grammar-neutral.md:30`,
   `:31`). They do not claim a REDRESS bypass: each is constrained to scalar
   parity, grammar-owned policy, generated callers, same-wave consumers, or
   non-JSON baseline routing. The packet repeatedly names the blocked adjacent
   routes from REDRESS 28/33, 54/55, 60-69, 72, 82/83, 106/107/108,
   116/117, and 119 (`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v12/research/p2/p2a-sota-teardown.md:135`;
   `restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v12/research/p2/p2e-parse-that-gaps.md:151`,
   `:282`, `:358`; `restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v12/research/p2/p2f-grammar-neutral.md:54`).
   CH3 accepts this only because the candidates are not JSON direct residual

   rejections.**
   P2-A C4/C5 require fresh non-JSON generated consumers and cannot become a
   JSON numeric-slot or container-tail retry (`restart/skinny/tranches/sk-v12/research/p2/p2a-sota-teardown.md:75`,
   `:87`, `:137`). P2-B's digit-span gate carries REDRESS 80/114 as an

   `:76`). P2-C's UDOT digit candidate keeps overflow, grammar policy, and

   explicitly blocks f64 fallback, mantissa widening, and numeric-slot reuse

   `:220`). This preserves REDRESS 80, 114, and 115.
