## Boundedness Finding

LOCKS and MASTER are realistically bounded as document CRUD only after the fold repairs above: LOCKS already has an antecedent per-hunk cost ledger in T-P3 3C, and MASTER has local cost/risk ranges in Ω-D plus a proposed-only diff. HANDOFF/MIGRATION and the skinny corpus are not yet bounded enough for G-Omega because they list broad rewrites without LOC ranges, file-touch counts, risk classes, or hard caps.

No artifact explicitly authorizes implementation work during document CRUD. The risk is classification drift: Ω-E and Ω-F name bench telemetry, decision-engine, primitive-manifest, and SIMD/ASM production obligations in doc-edit tables. Those obligations must be labeled as future S-P3/skinny implementation receivers, not as CRUD-4/CRUD-5 work.
