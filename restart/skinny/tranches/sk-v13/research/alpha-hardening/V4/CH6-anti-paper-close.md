# CH6 Anti-Paper-Close Challenge - SK-V13 Alpha V4

Disposition: ACCEPT.

Alpha V4 re-ran CH6 against the current SK-V13 packet with the V3 consolidated
result as context. No paper-close route is open. The packet still bars
support-only, deferred, scaffold-only, ordinary measured-reject, weaker-scoping,
old GO/slack, missing rolling-delta, and SIMD-policy-only closure. The remaining
risk is downstream S-P3 copying weaker scoping prose into `SPEC.md`, and the
handoff already marks that as a REVISE condition.

## Findings

### F1 - ACCEPT: close requires full ADMIT or architectural block

`SYNTHESIS.md` keeps the 2026-05-21 addendum as conflict authority over any
clause that treats CSS as one-row complete, treats `parse_only` as
diagnostic-only, permits JSON below strict sonic-rs, or allows ordinary
fixpoint close without architectural intrinsic-block evidence. Its close rule
is full ADMIT or architectural-level intrinsic-block proof for every remaining
row or feature; implementation-limited misses are reopens and force REJECT plus
immediate SK-V14 bracketing.

That blocks an ordinary measured-reject/fixpoint close. A measured rejection is
usable REDRESS evidence, but it is not close authority unless it proves an
architectural block.

### F2 - ACCEPT: support-only, deferred, and scaffold-only closure is barred

The synthesis requires every behavior wave to move at least one row toward SOTA
or prove architectural block, and it invalidates support-only landings unless
same-wave wired to a measured consumer. It also forbids deferring pinned work to
a future tranche except for the automatic bracket after rejected close.

The handoff operationalizes the same guard: support-only primitives, union
substrates, resolver infrastructure, or codegen paths reject without same-wave
measured consumers, and source/gate edits reject without telemetry and rolling
delta updates. Scoping documents can still contain scaffold/deferred planning
language, but the current packet does not let those labels become close-bearing
work.

### F3 - ACCEPT: weaker scoping precedence remains subordinate

The V3 fold remains intact. `SYNTHESIS.md`, `HANDOFF.md`, and the addendum
override weaker scoping prose. S-P3 must not inherit labels such as optional,
fallback, diagnostic, support-only, scaffold-only, or future-tranche for pinned
CSS/JSON/G2-G7 work. Those items must become admitted row targets,
architectural-block proofs, or user re-pin issues.

This is strong enough for CH6 because weaker scoping is not merely advisory; the
handoff makes inherited weaker labels a downstream REVISE condition.

### F4 - ACCEPT: old GO, slack, and REDRESS fixpoint outcomes cannot overclaim

The JSON close rule remains current-row and strict-comparator based. All 17
corpora across `parse_only`, `direct_to_struct`, and `real_typed_struct` must
beat sonic-rs strict on the same plane/corpus/strictness or carry per-row
architectural-block proof. REDRESS-119/120 are history only and do not block
fresh SK-V13 reopens.

Alpha-C carries the accounting consequence: the mandatory set is 51 JSON rows
plus 23 CSS parity features after the SK-V12 row, and REDRESS history or old
GO/slack outcomes are not closure authority under the addendum. Old A/GO rows
that miss the comparator-plus-one bar reopen rather than silently counting as
full SOTA.

### F5 - ACCEPT: rolling delta remains a close gate

`SYNTHESIS.md` requires `restart/skinny/ROLLING-SOTA-DELTA.md` for every JSON
row/plane and every CSS feature, with `row`, `plane`, `T1_current`, `T1_sota`,
`margin`, and `tranche_admitted`. It states the rolling table is a close gate,
not a status appendix; negative margins stay open unless architecturally
blocked, and margin regressions fail G7 unless covered by architectural-block
or user re-pin evidence.

The handoff also requires S-P3 to include rolling-delta production and gate
consumption in the concrete wave plan, and rejects source/gate edits without
telemetry and rolling delta updates.

### F6 - ACCEPT: G-SIMD-GRAMMAR-POLICY is not support-only closure

The current packet keeps `G-SIMD-GRAMMAR-POLICY` as a prerequisite for any wave
that wires `bbnf-simd` into CSS, union, JSON `parse_only`, or shared generated
code. The selected classifier must use the consuming grammar's
quote/escape/control policy or a no-string policy, with scalar parity,
JSON/CSS checkasm or differential coverage, same-wave measured row consumption,
no public substrate API, and no retained sidecar classifier state.

The pre-block list rejects non-JSON/shared `bbnf-simd` alphabet-only dispatch
unless that policy proves the selected path cannot inherit JSON
quote/escape/control constants. Alpha-E repeats the consumer-bound shape: the
policy must be consumed by a same-wave measured row and cannot add public
substrate/API shape or ship sidecar classifier state.

Therefore `G-SIMD-GRAMMAR-POLICY` is a legality and measurement gate. It cannot
be counted as a support-only landing, standalone proof, or policy-only tranche
close.

S-P3 must preserve these CH6 constraints in `SPEC.md` and
`DISPATCH-PROMPT.md`:

1. Behavior-wave exit requires named row movement or architectural-block proof.
2. Scaffold/support/checkasm/resolver/codegen-only landings are not
   close-bearing.
3. Ordinary measured-reject and REDRESS-119/120 fixpoint results are history or
   REDRESS evidence, not close authority.
4. Old A/GO and slack outcomes must be rechecked against the strict
   comparator-plus-one bar.
5. Weaker scoping labels are non-authoritative for pinned CSS/JSON/G2-G7 work.
6. `ROLLING-SOTA-DELTA.md` production and gate consumption remain mandatory
   close conditions.
7. `G-SIMD-GRAMMAR-POLICY` is mandatory for applicable `bbnf-simd` consumers
   and must be consumed by same-wave measured rows; it is not support-only
   closure.

With those carry-forward constraints, Alpha V4 CH6 accepts.
