or a BA/BB gate conflict, pause close and dispatch research, plan
augment/synthesis, and redress/redeployment agents. W5 - Terminal Close and
Handoff may publish a terminal close only after the synthesis is reflected in
FINAL, PROGRESS, trajectory, and inbound-link proof.
