## Scope

W1 verifies that B3.W0's revert restored the parser baseline, records the
parity bench numbers, and authors the close ceremony (FINAL.md + REMAINING-
TRAJECTORY + AY-II/PATH-FORWARD update + PROGRESS.md close entry). The
verification doubles as the B2-resume handoff: the `cargo xtask regen
--grammar bbnf` invocation that hung > 5 min on `21881591` is the canonical
parity probe.

escape clause expansion (W0-fix revert, then W0 base revert) dispatches
under orchestrator direction. Each scope expansion lands as additional
revert commits + an extended PROGRESS.md entry; W1 re-runs against the
expanded revert.

## Archaeology

The W1 verification suite is the parity probe equivalent of B2.W0.c's
"diagnostic narrowing" but executed on the post-revert substrate. The
diagnostic narrowing at B2.W0.c proved the wall lives in
`BbnfBootstrap::parse` itself; W1's three measurements (parser test,
compile_pipeline bench, xtask regen) verify the wall is gone post-B3.

The < 50 ms median ceiling on `compile_bbnf` is a 1-2 order-of-magnitude
relaxation over the AU baseline (911 µs) to allow for cold-cache and
nightly drift. A tighter ceiling would risk false-negatives under bench
variance. If the post-revert median lands closer to AU baseline (single-
digit milliseconds), B3 is over-floor; if it lands above 50 ms, B3 is
incomplete and the escape clause expansion dispatches.

The `cargo xtask regen` parity probe is the strongest single signal
because it runs the SAME code path B2.W0.c attempted — the IR pipeline
with the runtime parser. A successful regen here proves both that the
parser regression is gone AND that B2.W0.c's xtask body is correct as
authored. B2's W0.c re-execution becomes a re-run, not a re-design.
